## Seas

Roots: the 13 countries' bay / gulf / strait / lagoon / estuary / fjord /
sound / channel categories and the national bodies-of-water categories
(115 categories, 148 pages); the worldwide "List of straits" / "List of
gulfs" / "List of seas", "List of bays of Brazil" and "List of fjords of
Chile". 791 read as seas; **336 present** — the continent's named waters
were the bank's (the Río de la Plata, the Strait of Magellan, the Beagle
Channel, the Drake Passage, the Gulf of Guayaquil, San Matías, San Jorge,
Guanabara Bay, the Chilean fjords…); 4 taken, 15 fuzzy, 247 missing — the
worldwide lists' foreign rows and the sea concepts, cut by description; 98
under 213 views. No article pass (sea areas are rare; eight went in
unsized).

Went in: the **Argentine Sea** (940,000 km²; 1,005 views), **Mar de Grau**
(1,140,647 km²; Peru's sea by law, 304 views; pinned to the Pacific — no
coordinates on Wikidata), the **Gulf of Paria** (1,187; Trinidad–
Venezuela), the **Gulf of Venezuela** (487), the **Gulf of Darién** (578;
alias Gulf of Uraba), the **Gulf of Penas** (517; Chile; alias Golfo de
Penas), the **Bay of All Saints** (426; Salvador's; aliases Baia de Todos
os Santos, Todos os Santos Bay), **Le Maire Strait** (335; alias Strait of
Le Maire), **Bocas del Dragón** (304; alias Dragons Mouths), Rio's
**Rodrigo de Freitas Lagoon** (457; 2.2 km²).

Left out: Lake Maracaibo and Lagoa dos Patos (lake rows already — the sea
lists carry them), the Gulf of Fonseca (Central America's), 98 rows under
213 views (Reloncaví Sound, the Gulf of Corcovado, Bahía Blanca…).

## Deserts

Roots: the deserts of South America and the national desert categories,
the Atacama and Patagonian categories (12 categories, 90 pages); the
worldwide lists ("List of deserts of South America" does not exist). 150
read as deserts; **97 present** (the Atacama, Sechura, Patagonian, Monte,
Guajira deserts are the bank's); 38 missing — the concepts, the Atacama's
telescopes and geoglyphs, foreign deserts. One went in at the 213 floor:
the **Salvador Dalí Desert** (Bolivia; 274 views; alias Dali Desert).
Left out: the salt flats — **Salar de Atacama** (1,126 views) and **Salar
de Arizaro** (609) — and, noticed on the way, **Salar de Uyuni is in no
cohort at all** (the world's largest salt flat; a famous name the bank
cannot answer with — a desert row, a saltwater lake row, or a new kind: a
human's call); Pampa del Tamarugal (a plain), Lomas (a bioregion), the
Arid Diagonal (a climate zone).
