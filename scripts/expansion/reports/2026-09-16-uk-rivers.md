# United Kingdom + Ireland × rivers — coverage probe and fill (2026-09-16)

The first chunk of the Europe wave (HANDOFF.md: Western Europe + Scandinavia
& the Nordics, all categories per country, on branch `expansion`). Method:
every article under `Category:Rivers of England by county`, `…of Scotland by
council area`, `…of Wales by principal area`, `…of Northern Ireland by
county` and `…of the Republic of Ireland` (178 categories, 1,959 pages) plus
every link on the five national list pages and the UK list (2,598 links) —
3,175 candidate titles, 1,857 resolved to river / stream / burn / beck
articles — pushed through the game's matcher on the river cohort and checked
against each entry's `wikiTitle`. **Floor 50 km** (the US used 80: Britain's
rivers are short and famous — the Severn is 354 km, the Cam 64, the Forth
47), with Wikidata's P2043 first and, because Wikidata has no length for
1,496 of the 1,857 articles, every article's infobox read by
`article-size.mjs` (477 figures found; 10 disagreements, all resolved to the
article). Anything under the floor but with **1,000+ views a month** was
lifted back in (`famousViews`). Config: `probes/uk-rivers.json`.

## Decisions taken on the spot

- **Floor 50 km, not 80.** Britain's rivers are short: at 80 km the whole
  chunk would have been 20 rows and the Wensum, Yare, Waveney, Irwell, Thame
  and Bure (all 500–1,600 views a month) would have been left as traps.
- **Namesakes stay out (decision 5).** The `taken` list is the mirror image
  of the US one: River Dee (Aberdeenshire, 140 km, 1,522 views) is held by
  the Welsh Dee; Don (Yorkshire, 1,187 views; Aberdeenshire) by Russia's
  Don; Stour (eastern England, Dorset) by the Kent Stour; Avon (Hampshire,
  1,400 views) by the Bristol Avon; Derwent (Yorkshire) by Derbyshire's;
  Ouse (Sussex, 2,313 views) by Yorkshire's; Blackwater (Essex, Northern
  Ireland) by Florida's; Main (Antrim) by Germany's; South Esk (Angus) by
  Tasmania's; Dee (Ireland). Fourteen real rivers a British player might
  type; the day a name policy exists these are the first candidates.
- **Sizes as the article gives them** even where the figure looks generous
  (River Teise 112 km, River Teith 113 km — the infobox counts the
  headwaters); none crosses a size-prompt threshold.
- Robe (30 views), Owenmore (30), Ballisodare (30) went in: under the 50 km
  floor rule they qualify, and 30 views is above the cohort minimum (Mecaya,
  7).

## What the probe found on the way

- `probe.mjs` now tries the comma-stripped form of a British title ("River
  Ouse, Sussex" → "Ouse"), so a namesake reports as *taken* instead of
  *missing*; `chunk.mjs` drops the comma part when naming (a bare name that
  collides is refused by the fold with its reason).
- "List of rivers of Northern Ireland" has no links (a redirect); the NI
  category tree and the Ireland list cover it. "Longest rivers of the United
  Kingdom" is a section redirect (1 link).
- Wikipedia throttled from the first request (~5 s effective per request):
  category walk ~40 min, resolve ~15 min, article pass ~15 min, pass 2 ~5
  min.

## Judgment calls for a human

- **The British namesakes** (above). Decision 5 says not this wave; the list
  is the case for a name policy.
- **Welsh and Gaelic forms**: no `Afon X` / `Abhainn X` titles reached the
  floor this time (they are mostly short); when they do, the bare name should
  be an alias.
- Teise (112 km) and Teith (113 km) read long for what they are; the figure
  is the article's.

## Also fixed while this ran (`612aad1`)

Deriving the `North America` lake theme from article coordinates surfaced
five lakes whose article had no coordinates because it was the wrong article:
Lake Ypoá scored on *Lakes and rivers of Titan* (Saturn's moon; 2,222
views/mo), Lake Xiloá on *Volcanic crater lake* (the concept), Lake San Pablo
on *Khari Khari Lakes* (Bolivia), Lake Coatepeque on *Lake island* (the
concept) and Lagarfljót on *Lagarfljót Worm* (a cryptid). No English article
exists for Ypoá, Xiloá or San Pablo — dropped (an entry scores on its own
article or not at all). Coatepeque → `Coatepeque Caldera`, Lagarfljót →
`Lagarfljót` (both `WIKI_VERIFIED`). These came from the 2026-09-14 fill's
auto-title pass; the "no coordinates" list of any theme derivation is a
cheap wrong-article detector worth running on every cohort.
