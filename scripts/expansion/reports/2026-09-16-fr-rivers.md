# France, Belgium, Netherlands, Andorra, Monaco × rivers — coverage probe and fill (2026-09-16)

Third chunk of the Europe wave. Method: every article under `Category:Rivers
of France` (flat, 500+ pages), `…by region`, `…by department`, `…of
Metropolitan France`, `…of Belgium` (+ by province, by region), `…of the
Netherlands` (+ by province), `…of Andorra`, `…of Monaco` (152 categories,
948 pages) plus the links of the five national list pages — 1,526 candidate
titles, 940 resolved to river articles. **English Wikipedia holds far fewer
French river articles than German ones** (940 vs 3,345): the region and
department trees added 19 categories and not one page beyond the flat
category, and the five extra French list titles I guessed ("List of rivers
of France by length", "…of Brittany", "…of Normandy", "…of Corsica") don't
exist. **Floor 60 km**, `famousViews` 1000 (lifted nothing this time).
Wikidata sized 806 of the 940; the article pass read 283 (6 disagreements —
the decimal shift again: Geleenbeek 395 → 44.7, Mark (Dintel) 806 → 80.6,
Yerne 155 → 15.5). Config: `probes/fr-rivers.json`.

## Decisions taken on the spot

- **French Guiana out.** The category tree includes overseas France; the
  `subcat` regex excludes "Overseas" but the flat category and the lists
  carry the Guiana rivers directly. Dropped by description ("River in
  French Guiana"); the South America wave picks them up.
- **Two-letter names out** (Né). "Aff", "Arz", "Sée", "Vie", "Meu", "Lay",
  "Cure", "Dive", "Doux", "Touch" went in as the world names them (the US
  precedent: Big, Bad, Elk, New); they are three letters and more.

## Judgment calls for a human

- **Sée → "See"** and **Touch, Cure, Lay, Dive**: real rivers whose folded
  names are English words. Under the bare-English-word precedent they are
  in; each is a 30-view jackpot for a common word typed on a river round.
- The famous-typo class grows: "Drome" was a typo of Frome, "Chere" of Cher,
  "Maronne" of Garonne — each now lands on the real obscure river (same
  theme, same side of every threshold), exactly as with Thame/Thames.
- French Guiana's rivers, if the South America wave wants them under
  `France`'s flag on a city-style prompt — not applicable to rivers; they
  just need the `South America` theme.
