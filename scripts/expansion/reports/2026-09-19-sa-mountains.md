## Method

- 18 lists (seven of the 22 configured do not exist on enwiki — no
  national list for Venezuela or Guyana, no "highest mountains of South
  America"; replaced by *Tepui*, *Guiana Highlands* and the worldwide
  "List of mountains by elevation"): the national mountain and volcano
  lists of Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, the
  Ultras of South America, the Andes list. 2,181 read as mountains; **917
  present** (the Andes are the bank's already), 62 taken, 59 fuzzy, 1,143
  missing — 899 of them under 122 views and 163 foreign (the worldwide
  list: the Rockies, the Alps, the Himalaya, the Hawaiian cones).
- Sizes: Wikidata and the article agreed on 262, the article alone 6,
  Wikidata alone 12, `--sister=es,pt` 2; 44 unsized at 122+ views went in
  at `size` 0 (the tepuis and Guiana Highlands rows without an elevation).
- The hand-clean: `the Andes` by description (Argentina, Chile, Peru,
  Bolivia, Ecuador, Colombia, the Mérida Andes) with the Guiana Highlands
  (Venezuela's tepuis, Pico Naiguatá of the Coastal Range), the Sierra
  Nevada de Santa Marta (Pico Simón Bolívar), the Chilean Coast Range
  (Cerro Paranal, Cerro La Campana) and the Galápagos set to none;
  `volcanoes` by the description's word plus 22 by what the peak is
  (Galán, Socompa, Incahuasi, Aracar, Tipas, Antofalla, Acamarachi, Cerro
  Toco, Nevado San Francisco, Planchón-Peteroa, Sumaco, Melimoyu,
  Mocho-Choshuenco, Pichu Pichu, Acotango, Pomerape, Antuco, Lonquimay,
  Maipo, Solimana, Pululahua, the two Cerro Azuls). The description-rule
  false negatives kept by hand: Illiniza ("Volcanic mountains"), San Pedro
  ("One of the tallest active volcanoes"), Alcedo Volcano (no
  description; pinned in `WIKI_VERIFIED`).

- Concepts and ranges: Tepui, the Andean orogeny, the Sierras Pampeanas,
  the Nazca and Chile ridges, the Serranía del Perijá, the Farallones de
  Cali, the Andean natural region, Los Nevados park, Cerros de Mavecure
  (three hills); Cerro Guacha (a caldera whose Wikidata elevation is "5").

  Sierra Nevada de Lagunas Bravas, Cerro del Nacimiento…); 61 taken rows
  that are North American / Australian namesakes from the worldwide list.
