## Decided on the spot (all reported at the time)

- Floors: rivers 80 km for China and Mongolia, 60 km for Japan, the Koreas
  and Taiwan; lakes 25 km², islands 1 km², cities 50,000, mountains none;
  views floors as standing (30 / 61 / 91 / 122 / 213 / 91). Unsized rows at
  `size` 0: rivers at 122+ views (the Canada rule), islands at 122+, seas
  at 213+, mountains at 122+ (20 peaks without an elevation).
- **Three river themes created** (`China`, `Japan`, `Korea`), seeded with
  the incumbents — the wave's rows had nowhere to go otherwise. Adding a
  theme moves the daily draw (17 of 46 days differ from `main`, from
  2026-09-19 round 6); adding rows never had. Mongolia's and Taiwan's
  rivers carry no theme.
- Bare pinyin names are the bank's convention (Wu, Yu, Gan, You, Long,
  Xin, Zi, Mi…); within-chunk twins took the province; a qualified
  newcomer's bare key shadows an incumbent's alias, so Tuman (Xinjiang),
  Huang, Hong and Takeshima (Kagoshima) stayed out.
- Chinese prefecture-level cities carry the prefecture's population (the
  bank's Chongqing convention; 58 rows over 5 M); county-level and
  county-administered cities are cities; districts, towns and rural
  townships are not. Nine island- and country-named cities are qualified
  by prefecture (Okinawa (Japan), Usa (Oita), Tsushima (Aichi)…), not "X
  City": the audit found the "X City" form had no bare key and "USA" typed
  on a city round scored Usa.
- `article-size.mjs` reads zh / ja / ko infobox fields; a comma is a
  thousands mark there; a sister figure with a parenthetical qualifier
  ("238 km above the gorge") is a reach — Kezi went back to 900.
- `range-tag.mjs` has a Himalayas table (the Himalaya proper and its himals;
  the Karakoram, Kunlun, Tanggula, Nyenchen Tanglha, Hengduan, Pamir and
  Altai are not it); it reads the built `mountains.json`, so the pipeline's
  build runs first.
- Every Japanese island went into `Japan` (the Eastern Europe lesson); the
  volcanoes and salt lakes were tagged by infobox type after the audit.
