## Method

- Tree: the national lake and reservoir categories and their provincial
  subcategories (81 categories, 427 pages); the lists of lakes of China,
  Japan, Korea, Mongolia and Taiwan (the Taiwan list's 293 links are mostly
  townships). 1,045 candidates → 381 read as lakes; 39 present, 2 taken, 5
  fuzzy, 133 missing.
- Sizes: Wikidata had an area for 120; the article pass over all 283
  unsized-or-taken rows sized **121 more** (the Tibetan and Xinjiang lakes'
  infoboxes) and `--sister=zh,ja,ko,ru` 13; 88 have no figure anywhere and
  stayed out. 114 under 25 km² (Japan's crater lakes, Korea's reservoirs);
  four lifted back by 1,000+ views: Sun Moon Lake (7.9 km², 2,374 views),
  Lake Suwa (13.3), Lake Tazawa (25.9 — over anyway), Crescent Lake
  (Dunhuang) (0.01 km², 1,248 views).
- Views floor 61: 61 sized rows under it left out (the Tibetan salt lakes
  at 30 views, the Zhejiang reservoirs at 10–20).
- Themes: lakes have no East Asia theme; six salt lakes went into
  `saltwater` by description (Chaka, Xiechi, Zabuye, Ang Laren, Jilantai,
  Tangra Yumco).
