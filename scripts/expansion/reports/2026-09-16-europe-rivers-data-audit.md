## Confirmed

### C1. Kovda is a Russian river (nordic chunk)
- **Evidence.** `rivers.json` `river-kovda`: wikiTitle "Kovda", size 233, 30 views, lat 66.6908 lon 32.8694 (Kandalaksha Gulf, Murmansk Oblast); `titles.json`: "River in Russia". It came in from the Finnish tree/list (the Kovda basin's headwaters are Finnish) as a fuzzy trap ("Kovda -> Konda"). The nordic report's own rule dropped Maltan for the same reason (a Russian river a list dragged in) and the task's rule says Russia does not count.
- **Player sees.** "Kovda" accepted on Europe rounds (Russia's European rivers are in the theme anyway), so no wrong acceptance today; but the row is outside the wave's claim and the 233 km figure is the whole Kovda–Topozero–Pyaozero lake chain, not a river anyone names.
- **Fix.** `node scripts/expansion/drop.mjs river-kovda` (or keep with an explicit note in the nordic report). Severity: low.

### C2. Guadalevín 121 km is a decimal-shifted Wikidata figure (it-es chunk)
- **Evidence.** `river-guadalevin`: size 121, "wikidata only (no infobox figure)", raw Wikidata amount 121 (integer — the shape the eswiki "12,1" import takes). The Guadalevín is the headwater name of the Guadiaro (it cuts Ronda's gorge and becomes the Guadiaro below the Guadalcobacín confluence); the bank's `river-guadiaro` is **84 km** from its infobox. A headwater cannot be 37 km longer than the river it becomes. The it-es report itself named this row first among the 35 to spot-check.
- **Player sees.** A "121 km" river that is really ~12 km, below the 60 km floor; nothing crosses a size threshold either way.
- **Fix.** Drop `river-guadalevin` (the Odiel/Allaro rule: a Wikidata-only figure off by ten is not a figure), or verify live and re-size; at ~12 km it needs the 1,000-view exemption it does not have (244 views). Severity: medium.

### C3. Busento 90 km is not the Busento's length (it-es chunk)
- **Evidence.** `river-busento`: size 90, Wikidata-only (raw amount 90, integer); the infobox has only the mouth (39.2925,16.259 — Cosenza). The Busento rises on the Catena Costiera (Monte Cocuzzo) ~12 km SW of Cosenza and joins the Crati there, in the Crati's upper reaches; the bank's `river-crati` is **91 km**. A tributary entering near the head of a 91 km river cannot itself be 90 km, and 90 km over a ~12 km straight line is a sinuosity no mountain stream has. A "9,0" or a stray figure on Wikidata.
- **Player sees.** "Busento" (274 views — Alaric's burial river; it was the Busento -> Basento fuzzy trap) accepted with a figure that belongs to nothing. If the row is dropped, "Busento" goes back to being corrected to Basento (149 km, Basilicata) — same theme, same side of every threshold.
- **Fix.** Verify the length live (itwiki); if under 60 km, drop `river-busento` (let the Basento correction stand) or keep it as a famous-short exception by hand. Severity: low.

### C4. Eo is a two-letter name (it-es chunk)
- **Evidence.** `river-eo`: name "Eo", wikiTitle "Eo (river)", 92 km, 30 views (Galicia/Asturias). The fr chunk's stated rule (report, "Decisions taken on the spot"): "Two-letter names out (Né) … a jackpot for two keystrokes"; "Eo" went in the next chunk. The bank has 13 other two-letter rivers (Po, Ob, Aa, Xi, Wu, Yu, Da, Ca, Ma, Lo, Ou, Oi and Yí at 30 views — the Uruguayan Yí, from the 2026-09 fill), so the rule is the fr chunk's, not the bank's; Eo is only inconsistent with the decision taken one chunk earlier, and the two should be settled the same way.
- **Player sees.** "Eo" typed on any Europe/letter/size round is a 30-view jackpot for two keystrokes; it is also one edit from "Po" (a 2-letter name gets no fuzzy slack, so only a deliberate "Eo" lands — but a deliberate "Eo" is exactly the cheap jackpot the rule was written against).
- **Fix.** Drop `river-eo` for consistency with Né, or reverse the Né decision and add Né (the two are the same case). Severity: low.

### C5. Altaelva duplicates the fill's "Alta", whose row scores on the Ukrainian Alta (nordic chunk + 2026-09 fill)
- **Evidence.** `data-physical.mjs` line 2171 `Alta|187` sits in the Norwegian run (Numedalslågen, Namsen, Drammenselva, Gaula, Nidelva…): the Alta river of Finnmark. `data-wiki-titles.mjs` line 794 maps `river-alta` to "Alta River", which English Wikipedia redirects to **"Alta (river)"** — and the built `rivers.json` `river-alta` has wikiTitle "Alta (river)", lat 50.064 lon 31.461 (Pereiaslav, Ukraine — the 37 km Trubizh tributary), 30 views; `titles.json` under the key "Alta River": finalTitle "Alta (river)", **"River in Kyiv Oblast, Ukraine"**. The nordic probe then found "Altaelva" (240 km, 244 views, "River in Finnmark county, Norway") *missing* — the bank's "Alta" was a different title — and 871b259 folded it as `river-altaelva`. The Norwegian river is now in the bank twice in intent: as "Alta" (187 km, scoring on Ukraine) and as "Altaelva" (240 km). Found by the authored-neighbour sweep (`sweep-neighbours.out`: Alta is 1,691 km from its nearest neighbour).
- **Player sees.** "Alta" or "Alta River" scores a 30-view jackpot on a Ukrainian stream's rarity and coordinates; "Altaelva" scores the real river; both count in one run (two rows, no duplicate detection between them). On a coordinate-derived theme "Alta" would leave Europe's Nordic set.
- **Fix.** Drop `river-alta` and give `river-altaelva` the aliases `"Alta"`, `"Alta River"` (`add-alias.mjs`); that keeps the article figure (240 km) and the 244 views. (Or re-point `river-alta` to "Altaelva" and drop the new row — but the 187 km figure has no source.) Severity: high (a duplicate that pays a jackpot).

### C6. Nera (the fill's Italian Nera, 116 km) scores on Nera (Danube) — Eder class, surfaced by the it-es taken list
- **Evidence.** `it-es-rivers.json` lists "Nera (Tiber)" (116 km article, 213 views) as *taken* by the bank's Nera. `data-physical.mjs` line 1663 `Nera|116` is in the Italian run (Basento, Crati, Sele, Calore, Volturno, Liri, Aniene, **Nera**, Velino, Fiora, Ombrone…) and `wikitext-cache.json` "Nera (Tiber)" has `length_km = 116` — the row *is* the Tiber's Nera (the Marmore Falls river). `data-wiki-titles.mjs` line 553 maps `river-nera` to **"Nera (Danube)"** (39534ba's resolver): `rivers.json` `river-nera` is 116 km with coordinates 44.83,21.35 (the Danube confluence in the Banat) and the Romanian article's 122 views. The it-es report treated it as a namesake ("held by the Romanian Nera, 143 km") — but the bank's row carries the Italian figure, not the Romanian one.
- **Player sees.** "Nera" accepted with the Italian river's length and the Romanian river's rarity and place; a coordinate-derived theme puts it in Romania.
- **Fix.** `scripts/data-wiki-titles.mjs` line 553: `"river-nera": "Nera (Tiber)"`; re-fetch views (213) and coordinates (42.44,12.41). Size 116 stays. Severity: medium.

### C7. Arda (the fill's Bulgarian Arda, 292 km) scores on Arda (Italy) — Eder class, surfaced by the it-es taken list
- **Evidence.** `it-es-rivers.json` lists "Arda River (Douro tributary)" (Portugal, unsized) as *taken* by the bank's Arda. `data-physical.mjs` line 1908 `Arda|292` is in the Bulgarian run (Maritsa, Iskar, Yantra, Osam, Vit, Ogosta, Struma, Mesta, **Arda**, Tundzha, Kamchiya) — the Maritsa's Arda (Kardzhali; ~290 km). `data-wiki-titles.mjs` line 667 maps `river-arda` to **"Arda (Italy)"**: `rivers.json` `river-arda` is 292 km with coordinates 45.03,10.05 (the Po near Cremona — the ~55 km Emilian stream), "River in Italy", **30 views** (`sweep-neighbours.out`: 1,077 km from its nearest authored neighbour).
- **Player sees.** "Arda" is a 30-view jackpot carrying a 292 km figure that belongs to the Bulgarian river; on Europe rounds it is accepted either way, but the rarity is wrong by an order of magnitude and the place is wrong.
- **Fix.** `data-wiki-titles.mjs` line 667: `"river-arda": "Arda (Maritsa)"` (verify the exact title live); re-fetch views and coordinates. Severity: medium.

## Judgment calls

### Check 1 — place
- **Koitajoki** ("River in the countries of Finland and Russia", 200 km) and **Lotta** ("River in Finland, Russia", 190 km): Finnish rivers that cross the border (the Koitajoki loops through Russia and back; the Lotta/Lutto rises in Finland and ends in Russia). Coordinates in the Nordic box; kept.
- **Schipbeek** (de chunk, 85 km, "Watercourse in the Netherlands"): a partly canalised stream from Germany to the IJssel; in the de chunk because its upper course is German. A river to the player; the only row whose description is not "River/Tributary".
- **Eisbach** ("Manmade river in Munich", 2 km, 3,227 views): the de report's deliberate famous-short pick; an artificial channel. Noted, not a defect.
- **Chiers** (de chunk, mouth 49.66,4.99 at Sedan, France; "River in Luxembourg, Belgium and France") and **Rabnitz** (de chunk, mouth 47.71,17.57 near Győr; source at Hollenthon, Lower Austria; Hungarian Répce; 120 km Wikidata-only with a 96 km straight line — right): fine.
- 13 rows have no page coordinate (Elz, Schwentine, Helme, Adaja, Deva, Bernesga, Clitunno, Najerilla, Cega, Duratón, Esgueva, Gallo, Gévora, Sorbe): descriptions are the right rivers; invisible to any coordinate-derived theme (the UK audit's class).

### Check 2 — figures
- All 417 rows with an infobox length carry that figure (within rounding); 46 are Wikidata-only (6 de, 3 fr, 27 it-es, 10 nordic; list in `audit-rows.out`). Judged one by one against the river's course (source-to-mouth straight line where the infobox has both, the main river's own bank figure, the raw Wikidata amount — a decimal in the raw amount, e.g. Boulogne 86.5, Soeste 71.65, Trabancos 85.86, means a measured figure, not a shifted import): plausible except C2, C3 and **Clitunno** (60 km, exactly the floor; Fonti del Clitunno 42.825,12.767 to Bevagna is ~17 km; 60 would be the Clitunno–Timia–Teverone system; a live look would settle it — if it is under 60 it goes).
- The straight-line check flagged Cosson (96 km, "source" 263 km away) and Børselva (76 km, "source" 209 km away): both are longitude typos in the articles' `source1_coordinates` (Cosson 2°14′W for E; Børselva 30°35′E for ~25°E); the lengths are the infoboxes' own.
- Nothing is within 20% of 500 km; the largest new rows are Västerdal/Österdal 300, Lainio 266, Saar 246, Altaelva 240.
- Re-points: Eder 177 (infobox 176), Aller 211 (215), Thur 130 (135), Orne 170 (170) — rounding; views and coordinates are the new articles' (Eder 18 -> 335, Aller 30 -> 335, Thur 30 -> 183, Orne 61 -> 244). Clean.
