Branch `expansion-2`. Probe `scripts/expansion/probes/sa-cities.json` (16
national lists with `listCountry`; floor 50,000; **views floor 213 for this
wave's cities — decided on the spot, see below**); chunk files in

description (French Guiana's row is France's).

## The floor decision

The 91-views floor put the city jackpot share at **11.23%** (680 of 6,055;
the line stays 312.6 views): 316 of the wave's 617 rows sit under the line
— Brazil's, Colombia's and Argentina's municipalities of 50–150k that few
English readers look up. The share had gone 1.38% → 4.74% (East Asia) →
6.72% (West and Central Asia) and the user has the question open; a cohort
at 11% is outside the band every other cohort keeps (1.2–3.4%), so this
wave's cities took a **213 floor**: 173 rows dropped (Brazil 72, Colombia
32, Argentina 31, Chile 11, Paraguay 9, Venezuela 7, Ecuador 5, Peru 3,
Bolivia 3 — the ids are in `work/sa8-under213-ids.txt`, the rows with
their views in `work/sa-cities.json`), share **8.65%** (509 of 5,884). The
other knobs, for the user: a 313 floor for this wave leaves 6.34%; the
same 213 floor applied back to the East Asia and West and Central Asia
city rows would take the cohort near 5%. The floor is a wave decision,
not a rule change: the 91 floor stands in HANDOFF until the user picks.

## Method

- 16 lists (the Brazil municipalities list does not exist; the two Brazil
  city lists share 511 links; Colombia's plain list has 1,255). 4,121
  candidates → 2,539 read as cities; 153 present, 37 taken, 50 fuzzy, 583
  missing; 1,017 under 50,000; 699 unsized.
- The hand-clean keeps a row whose description names a city / town /
  municipality / commune and does not start with a division word; the
  state-capital descriptions ("Capital city of Espírito Santo") were kept
  by hand (Vitória, Boa Vista, Rio Branco, Quibdó, Chillán, Viedma,
  Tacuarembó, Melo, San Felipe); Santiago's comunas (Las Condes, Maipú, La
  Florida, San Bernardo…) are a city's districts and stayed out, Chile's
  provincial comunas that are towns went in (Alto Hospicio, San Felipe,
  Buin, Colina…); Vicente López (a Buenos Aires partido) went in as the
  municipality it is (pinned in `WIKI_VERIFIED`); Sucre (Bolivia's
  constitutional capital) and the national capitals were refused by the
  fold.
- Populations are Wikidata's: Brazil's are the município's (the
  administrative-city convention); Santo Domingo, Chile carried the
  Dominican capital's 1,029,110 (a Wikidata slip — dropped) and Palermo,
  Huila 800,000 for a town of 30k (dropped).
