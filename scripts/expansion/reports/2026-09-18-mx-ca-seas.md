`mx-ca-deserts.json`; chunk files in `work/folded/mx-ca/` (`new-seas-mxca.txt`,

## Seas — method

- Tree: Canada's bays / straits / gulfs / sounds / inlets / fjords /
  channels / estuaries / seas by province, Mexico's bays / gulfs / lagoons,
  the seas of the Arctic and Pacific (95 categories, 907 pages); the
  worldwide lists of straits / gulfs / bays / seas plus the Canadian lists.
  2,135 candidates → 1,365 read as seas; 476 missing. Almost none has an
  area: `--allow-no-figure` at the standing 213-views floor; the article pass
  over the 327 in scope gave 26 areas.
- **The worldwide lists dragged in 209 rows outside the wave** (Ha Long Bay,
  the Tsushima Strait, Walvis Bay, the Gulf of Venezuela, Hauraki Gulf…):
  left for their own waves, as the Europe wave did; the chunk was cut to the
  rows whose description or title names Canada, a province, Mexico, a
  Mexican state, the Canadian Arctic — or a North American neighbour the
  US wave had missed (Nantucket Sound, Charleston Harbor, Norton Sound).

## Deserts

Mexico's deserts were all present (93 of the probe's 153); Canada has none
by category. Three badlands went in at size 0: Cheltenham Badlands
(Ontario, 1,339 views), Big Muddy Badlands (Saskatchewan–Montana), Rock
Creek Badlands (Saskatchewan). `--check` now accepts "badlands" for a
desert. The Badlands Guardian (a landform that looks like a head) was left
out; so was Painted Desert (South Australia), the one taken row.
