# United States × lakes — coverage probe and fill (2026-09-16)

Second chunk of the US scouring. Method as for the rivers
(`reports/2026-09-16-us-rivers.md`): `Category:Lakes of the United States by
state` and `Category:Reservoirs in the United States by state` (state level
only), 54 list pages, 10,898 candidate titles → 5,043 lake/reservoir
articles → the matcher on the lake cohort. **Floor 25 km² (≈10 sq mi)**,
Wikidata P2046 first, then every article's infobox (`article-size.mjs`);
**1,000+ views/mo lifts a lake back over the floor**. Config
`probes/us-lakes.json`.

## Outcome (same day)

- **`e2e8c1b` — 378 rows**: 317 lakes and reservoirs ≥ 25 km² plus 61
  famous small ones (Chaubunagungamaug 17,320 views, Peigneur 15,494, Bde
  Maka Ska, Lake Las Vegas, Lady Bird Lake, Jenny Lake, Convict Lake, Quake
  Lake, Lake Merritt, the Central Park Reservoir, Alcohol and Drug Abuse
  Lake…). Fuzzy traps fixed: Lake Sinclair → St. Clair, Lake Abert → Albert,
  Rend Lake → Red Lake, Belton → Elton, Heron Lake → Huron, Laguna Madre →
  Laguna Verde.
- **Two existing rows re-pointed**: "Lake Crescent" (20 km², Washington)
  scored on Crescent Lake (Oregon); "Jackson Lake" (100 → 103 km², Wyoming)
  on Jackson Lake (Georgia). Same wrong-disambiguation class as the five
  rivers.
- The 45 new "X Reservoir" rows carry "X" as an alias, because `reservoir`
  is not a filler word and people say "Elephant Butte", "Sam Rayburn".
- 2 rows `WIKI_VERIFIED` (no short description); Upper Peoria Lake dropped
  ("Section of the Illinois River").
- **Dig #1's pin moved.** Lakes under 100 km² went from 52% to 60.0% of the
  cohort — one entry over `MAX_ELIGIBLE_SHARE` — so `drawModifier` now
  refuses "Name a lake smaller than 100 km²" and the same seed draws
  "larger than" for round 12. `tests/prompts.test.js` updated knowingly; the
  knob is the user's (see the decisions below).
- Bank 10,621; lake cohort 1,168 (was 790). Cohort minimum unchanged.

## Left out on purpose

- **Great Lakes** (64,228 views): typed, it is autocorrected to Great Lake
  (Tasmania) — an ugly trap, but a row "Great Lakes" would be five lakes in
  one. Best fix is engine-side (refuse to correct a plural onto a singular
  namesake) or an alias-free "not a lake" nudge; left for the user.
- Former lakes (Agassiz, Bonneville, Manly), Lake Michigan–Huron, Hampton
  Roads, the Apostle Islands, Northwest Angle, Tidal Basin, groups (Belgrade
  Lakes, Warner Lakes, Alkali Lakes, Twin Lakes, Fish River chain), Sites
  Reservoir (proposed), Lake Mohawk NJ / Seboomook Lake / Lake and Peninsula
  Borough (places), Sky Lakes Wilderness, Western Maine Lakes and Mountains.
- The 12 name-taken lakes, per the standing rule.

## Judgment calls left for a human

- **`MAX_ELIGIBLE_SHARE` vs the lake size prompt.** Every wave adds more
  small lakes than large ones (Europe's alpine lakes next), so "smaller than
  100 km²" is gone for good at 0.6 unless the knob moves (0.65 or 0.7) or
  `SIZE_RULES.lake` gains a 25 km² tier.
- **`reservoir` as a filler word** would replace the 45 aliases and make the
  8 older "X Reservoir" rows reachable bare too, at the cost of a loose-form
  collision check (Silver Lake Reservoir vs Silver Lake, Clear Lake
  Reservoir vs Clear Lake).
- No lake theme covers North America (`themes.js` has saltwater, the Great
  Lakes, Africa, the Alps, the British Isles, Scandinavia), so the 378 rows
  answer plain, size, letter and the Great-Lakes-adjacent prompts only. A
  "North America" or "the United States" lake theme is fair game if asked.
