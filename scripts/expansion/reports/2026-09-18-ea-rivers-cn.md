## Method

- Tree: `Rivers of China` (its provincial subcategories at depth 3),
  `Rivers of Mongolia`, `Rivers of Hong Kong`, `Rivers of Macau` — 40
  categories, 469 pages; the lists of rivers of China (605 links, a third of
  them army / project / concept pages), Mongolia and Hong Kong. 1,047
  candidates → 482 read as rivers; 72 present, 17 taken, 24 fuzzy, 201
  missing.
- Sizes: Wikidata had a length for 156; the enwiki article pass sized 54
  more and overrode 15 (Chishui 523 vs 436, Kaidu 610 vs 497, Shule 861 vs
  670…); `--sister=zh,ru,ja` sized 38 (zhwiki's `length` field carries
  `{{Convert|198|km}}` or "108公里" — read either way; the Chinese comma is
  a thousands mark now). 143 rivers have no figure anywhere; 19 of them at
  122+ views went in at `size` 0 (Shaksgam, Sengge Zangbo, Ganga Chhu,
  Maquan, Molcha, Haolai…).
- Views floor 30; 13 rows under it left out (Zelter, Xianshui, Taoer,
  Futun, Konqi…).

## Left out, and why

- The list's non-rivers: the PLA's three branches, the United States Army,
  River, the Yellow River civilization, the South–North Water Transfer
  Project, Intensive farming, Three Parallel Rivers, Kai Tak Nullah;
  "Sichuan River" (zhwiki's 6,300 km is the Yangtze's — it is the Yangtze's
  Sichuan reach).
- Other waves' rivers from the list: Nepal's Trishuli, Sunkoshi,
  Bhotekoshi, Tamakoshi; India's Lohit, Subansiri, Jadh Ganga; Bhutan's Kuri
  Chhu; Vietnam's Nho Quế, Bằng, Kỳ Cùng, and the Thao (the Red River's
  Vietnamese reach); Myanmar's N'Mai and Shweli; Russia's Razdolnaya.
- The disputed Ladakh / Arunachal rivers (Galwan — 65 km, under the floor
  anyway — Chang Chenmo, Charding Nullah, Sumdorong Chu, Nyamjang Chu);
  Shaksgam (Chinese-administered) is in.
- Tuman (Xinjiang) (83 km, 30 views): its bare key shadowed the Tumen's
  alias "Tuman" (a qualified newcomer's bare key beats an incumbent's
  alias); dropped. Huang (the Huai tributary, 140 km, 30 views): "Huang"
  typed would have been it rather than the Yellow River; dropped. (An alias
  "Huang" on the Yellow River made it eligible for "short name" rounds
  through the alias — the Micronesia-class letter-rule bug the user has
  noted — and broke a daily-server test; the alias is not carried.)
- Qi River (tributary of Yangtze River) (30 views, no place to qualify it
  by); 13 rows under 30 views; 25 under 80 km; 124 unsized under 122 views.
