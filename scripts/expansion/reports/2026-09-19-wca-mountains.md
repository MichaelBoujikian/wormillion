Branch `expansion-2`. Probe `scripts/expansion/probes/wca-mountains.json`
(lists only, no elevation floor, views floor 122); chunk files in
`work/folded/wca/` (`new-mountains-wca4.txt`, `new-mountains-ns-wca4.txt`,

## Method

- **Twelve of the 34 configured lists do not exist** (Iraq, Kazakhstan,
  Kyrgyzstan, Yemen, Israel, Jordan, Cyprus; "highest mountains of Turkey",
  and the Caucasus / Pamir / Tian Shan / Hindu Kush lists) — enwiki has no
  national mountain list for those countries; a prefix and a full-text
  search found nothing better. Three real lists replaced them: "List of
  highest mountain peaks in the Arab World", "List of the highest major
  summits of Azerbaijan", "List of volcanoes in Afghanistan". Kazakhstan's,
  Kyrgyzstan's and the Caucasus's big peaks come through the two Ultras
  lists (West Asia, Central Asia) and were mostly incumbents already (61
  present of 267 read as mountains; 946 skipped).
- The "List of volcanoes in Syria/Yemen/Saudi Arabia" pages are mostly
  concept links (Stratovolcano, Fumarole, the Global Volcanism Program) —
  cut by the hand-clean's description rule.
- Sizes: Wikidata and the article agreed on 55; the article alone sized 6
  (Mount Uhud 1,077, Ayr 955, Jabal Daka 2,500, Oshtorankuh 4,050) and
  overrode Wikidata's 0 for Takur Ghar (3,191); 8 are Wikidata's alone
  (Mount Arafat 454, Abu Qubays 420, Honaz, Tmolus); two went in at `size`
  0 (Jabal Maqla — 2,326 m by other sources, no infobox; Al-Qarah Mountain,
  the Al-Ahsa mesa). The ten sister wikis sized nothing new (the unsized
  rows at 122+ views have no sitelinks).
- Views floor 122: 115 sized rows under it left out — Iran's Alborz and
  Zagros peaks at 30 views (Azad Kooh 4,355, Shaneh-Kuh 4,465, Kuh-e Shah
  4,402, Sialan, Kholeno 4,387), Afghanistan's Hindu Kush at 30–91 (Koh-e
  Keshni Khan 6,745, Kohe Mondi 6,234, Sakar Sar 6,271, Lunkho e Dosare
  6,901, Kuh-e Safed Khers 5,326), Tajikistan's Moscow Peak 6,785 and
  Patkhor 6,083 at 61, Kyrgyzstan's Pik Skalisty / Tandykul, Turkey's Erek /
  Medetsiz, Mongolia's Ikh Bogd / Jargalant Khairkhan / Türgen (East Asia's,
  through the Central Asia Ultras list), Syria's and Saudi Arabia's hills.
  A 61 floor would have brought 26 more Central Asian 6,000-metre peaks in.

## For a human

- Mount Nemrut now carries the qualifier Adiyaman; its id is
  `mountain-mount-nemrut-adiyaman` (the gap-check pins and any daily that
  drew the old id were checked by the pipeline).
- Ailama's description says Russia; the article puts it on the border, in
  Svaneti's section of the range. Janga / Tebulosmta / Shani are the same
  class (Georgia–Russia border peaks) and went in on the Georgian side.
- The Hindu Kush and Pamir 6,000-metre peaks at 30–91 views are the wave's
  biggest unsized-by-fame gap; a lower mountain floor for Central Asia is
  a human's call.
