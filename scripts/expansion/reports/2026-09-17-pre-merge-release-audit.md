## Section 5. The client: how the bank loads, caching, weight (verified)

- `src/index.html` loads everything with plain `<script src="...">` tags (lines 184-201: `data/bank.js`, `data/themes.js`, then the 14 engine files, `main.js`), **no query string, no version, no cache-buster, no service worker, no manifest, no `_headers`/`_redirects`** anywhere in `src/` or `netlify/`. `index.html` itself is unchanged by the merge (it is not in the diff).
- Live headers (curl 2026-09-17): **GitHub Pages** sends `Cache-Control: max-age=600` (+ ETag, gzip) on every file; **Netlify** sends `Cache-Control: public,max-age=0,must-revalidate` (+ ETag). So on Netlify every page load revalidates every file: after the deploy a page load is all-new or (for the seconds a deploy takes to propagate) all-old - Netlify deploys are atomic - and a mixed engine/bank page is only possible if the load straddles the flip. On Pages a returning player can reuse any file for up to 10 minutes after their last load; files fetched in one page load expire together, so the usual outcome is all-old (within 10 min) or all-new (after), but a browser that evicted the 2 MB `bank.js` and kept the small engine files (or the reverse) can build a **mixed page** for those 10 minutes.
- What a mixed page does (measured with the two engines and two banks, `draws.cjs`): for 2026-09-17 the live draw is `e65635c8` on old+old and on new+new alike, but `cc1197cf` on new-engine+old-bank and on old-engine+new-bank. A mixed page therefore plays a **different fifteen prompts** than everyone else that day and its record's `draw` is refused by the server with 409 `draw-mismatch` (the comparison block stays hidden for that player for that day; no score is wrong, nothing local breaks). On 2026-09-18 all four combinations draw `2a89a1bc`, so a mixed page there would be harmless. The mix cannot outlast 10 minutes on Pages and cannot happen on Netlify; no fix needed for this release, but a `?v=` on the script tags (or a version constant compared against the bank) is the cheap future guard - see the checklist.
- **Weight and parse** (`zlib` in Node 24): `bank.js` 1,971,907 bytes raw, gzip-6 276,615 (gzip-9 268,047; brotli 218,850) vs the live 1,260,546 / 181,292 (Pages served the old one at 187,383 bytes on the wire). Whole page (html + css + bank + themes + 15 js): 2,312,440 raw, ~392 KB gzip, ~319 KB brotli. Both hosts gzip. Evaluating `bank.js` takes 42 ms and `createBank` 130-150 ms in Node on this machine - well under a second even at a phone's 3-5x. SPEC 8's "well under 1 MB" was already exceeded by the 9,300 bank (HANDOFF notes it); the merge takes the wire weight from ~250 KB to ~390 KB. Not a blocker; noted for the docs.
