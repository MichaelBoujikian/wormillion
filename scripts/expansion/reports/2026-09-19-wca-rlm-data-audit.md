### F7. The Turkish dotless ı (U+0131) is not folded by the matcher: seven new rows typed as Wikipedia spells them — "Lake Çıldır", "Lake Işıklı", "Çıldır", "Işıklı" — are unrecognized, and "Lake Acıgöl", "Lake Balık", "Tahtalı Dağı", "Kızılkaya", "Biga Çayı" land only as fuzzy corrections

- Evidence: `matching.normalize("Lake Çıldır")` → "lake cıldır", `normalize("Işıklı")` → "isıklı" (Bash run above); wca-battery.mjs anomalies: "Lake Çıldır" / "Çıldır" / "Lake Işıklı" / "Işıklı" unrecognized on the lake round, "Lake Acıgöl", "Acıgöl", "Lake Balık", "Balık", "Tahtalı Dağı", "Kızılkaya", "Biga Çayı" `(corrected)`; "Kızılırmak" unrecognized on both stacks.

- Fix (engine, one line): in src/js/matching.js normalize(), fold `ı` → `i` alongside ø/æ/œ/ł (`.replace(/ı/g, 'i')`), and the same in `variantsOf`/letter handling if it normalizes separately; then the battery above passes with no data change. - Severity: wrong refusal of seven new rows' own titles (and nine incumbents'); pre-existing gap, doubled by the wave.
