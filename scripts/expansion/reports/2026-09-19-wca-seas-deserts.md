## Seas

Roots: the 25 countries' bay / gulf / strait / lagoon categories and the
Persian Gulf, Red Sea, Caspian, Black Sea, Marmara, Aegean, Gulf of Oman,
Arabian Sea, Gulf of Aden, Indian Ocean seas categories (119 categories,
395 pages); the worldwide "List of straits" / "List of gulfs" / "List of
seas" and "List of bays of Turkey". 820 read as seas; **337 present** — the
region's named waters were already the bank's (the Gulf of Izmit, Antalya,
Iskenderun, Izmir, Gökova, Suez, Aqaba, Bahrain, Masirah, Hormuz, Bab-el-
Mandeb, Bosphorus, Dardanelles…). 7 taken, 14 fuzzy, 289 missing, of which
the worldwide lists' foreign rows (Guantánamo, Hạ Long, Port Jackson,
Spencer Gulf, the Irminger Sea…) and the sea concepts (Tide, Fjord, Ria,
Seiche, Oceanic trench…) were cut by description; the wave's own missing
rows at 213+ views were six. No article pass was run (sea areas are rare
and the six went in unsized).

Went in: **Straits of Tiran** (5,114 views; the Gulf of Aqaba's mouth,
between Sinai and Saudi Arabia; alias Strait of Tiran), **ANZAC Cove**
(1,796; Gallipoli; pinned in `WIKI_VERIFIED` — Wikidata describes the
battle site), **Suvla** (578; the bay north of it; alias Suvla Bay),
**Bay of Baku** (304; 50 km²), **Kuwait Bay** (244), **Anzali Lagoon**
(213; Gilan's Caspian lagoon). The two Caspian rows carry `[]` in
`OCEAN_OVERRIDES`; the Gallipoli pair sit in the Atlantic (the Aegean's
box) like the Dardanelles.

Left out: the Turkish Straits (the Bosporus + Dardanelles pair — a group,
like the Danish Straits, both out), Coral Bay, Cyprus (1,278 views — a
resort beach; the bank's Coral Sea holds the loose "Coral Bay"), Half Moon
Bay, Saudi Arabia (152 views), Türkmenbaşy Gulf (183), Dead Kultuk (152),
Clarence Strait (Iran) (152), Gulf of Fethiye (122), Lake Küçükçekmece (a
lagoon lake, 16 km² — a lake row if anything), the Volga Delta, Sea of the
Khazars (a historical name), the oil and gas fields, the earthquakes, the

## Deserts

Roots: the deserts of Asia and the 16 national desert categories (23
categories, 63 pages); the worldwide lists ("List of deserts of Asia" does
not exist). 148 read as deserts; **98 present**; 33 missing, of which 8
are the wave's: **Sakhir** (731 views; Bahrain's desert; alias Sakhir
Desert) and **Red Sand** (335; the dunes east of Riyadh; alias Red Sand
Dunes) went in at `size` 0; **Dasht-e Leili** (183; Jowzjan), **Aral
Karakum Desert** (152; 40,000 km²), **Jiddat al-Harasis** (122; Oman) and
Taukum (30) were left out under a **213 views floor** — the desert
cohort's jackpot share is the standing open question (8.28% before this
probe, 8.16% after; the three 122–183-view rows would have put it near

other way — a human's call which rule the cohort keeps). Makran (a coastal
region), the Desert of Paran (biblical), Umm al Samim (a quicksand sabkha)
are not deserts by the cohort's sense; the rest of the missing rows are
foreign (Sleeping Bear Dunes, Katpana, Sarfaranga, El Djouf, Nyiri, Grand
Bara, Lompoul, Sarykum…).
