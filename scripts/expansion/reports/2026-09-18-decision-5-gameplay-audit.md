### C1 (medium, engine) — after a US state capital is scored, the second typing of its name is the capital nudge, not the next namesake

`d5-check1b-out.txt`. A run with two plain city rounds (or a plain and a North America round):

| typed twice | 1st | 2nd (want) | 2nd (got) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Boston | Boston (Massachusetts) 112,689 v | Boston (Lincolnshire) 11,263 v | `elsewhere: capital-boston` — **"Boston is a capital city — this round wants a city that isn't a national capital."** |
| Columbus | Columbus (Ohio) 49,952 | Columbus (Georgia) 13,333 | "Columbus is a capital city — …" (also on "Name a non-capital city in North America") |
| Madison | Madison (Wisconsin) 33,332 | Madison (Alabama) 4,079 | "Madison is a capital city — …" |
| Richmond | Richmond (Virginia) 39,055 | Richmond (California) 7,945 | "Richmond is a capital city — …" |
| Jackson | Jackson (Mississippi) 26,665 | Jackson (Tennessee) 6,758 | "Jackson is a capital city — …" |
| Albany | Albany (New York) 36,284 | Albany (Georgia) 6,271 | "Albany is a capital city — …" |

Why: `run.js` `famousElsewhere` compares the *settled* entry's views with the capital cohort's exact holder. On the second typing `matchAnswer` settles on the first unused namesake (Boston (Lincolnshire), 11,263 v), and `capital-boston` (112,689 v, the same place the player just scored as `city-boston-massachusetts`) is 10× that, so the guard fires. The player reads a nudge that is wrong twice over: Boston is not a national capital (the city cohort holds it, and the round just gave points for it), and the design promises "a second typing takes the other". Springfield / Concord / Augusta / Columbia escape only because their namesakes' views are within 3× of the capital's.

Fix (`src/js/run.js`, `famousElsewhere`): measure the ratio against the bare name's most-viewed holder in the *current cohort*, not the settled entry — the in-cohort fame of the name is what says whether the player could have meant this cohort:

```js
const top = Math.max(...matching.idsAt(current.cohort.lookup, matching.normalize(entry.name)).map((id) => current.cohort.byId.get(id).magnitude));
if (!best || best.magnitude < NAMESAKE_FAME_RATIO * top) return null;
```

With that, the six designed nudges are unchanged (Athens (Georgia) is the only Athens in the city cohort, so `top` is its own views) and the second Boston is Boston (Lincolnshire). Add a test beside `tests/namesakes.test.js` "a plain round takes the most-viewed namesake; a second typing is the other one": Boston twice on `{category:'city'}`.

### C2 (medium, engine) — the fame guard is switched off on letter, size, flag and theme rounds, and the nudge `main` gave there is now a silent score

`run.js` `famousElsewhere`: `if (current.constrained && !current.region) return null;`. The comment's rationale — "only when the famous holder would have fitted the round" — holds for a region round (the capital Athens cannot be meant on "in North America") but not for a letter round: the famous holder has the *same bare name*, so it fits "starts with A" / "has an N" exactly as the namesake does, and a player who types "Athens" there has told the game nothing about which one they mean — the same situation as the plain round, where the design keeps the nudge for the reason HANDOFF gives ("'Athens' on a plain city round would quietly score a 127,000-person Georgia town for a player who meant Greece"). The table above is that quiet score: on `main` every one of these typings was the nudge; now it is points, up to 68% obscure for "Greece" on "starts with G". `tests/namesakes.test.js` line 214 pins `startsA('Athens')` to Athens (Georgia), so this is a deliberate choice the lead should re-take rather than a slip; the audit's view is that it is wrong for the player and a regression against the live site.

Fix (`src/js/run.js` `famousElsewhere`): drop the `current.constrained && !current.region` early return, keeping the region test that follows — the guard then runs on every round except a region round the famous holder does not carry, which restores `main`'s outcome on letter / size / flag / theme rounds and keeps the design's acceptance of Boston (Lincolnshire) on "in Europe". With C1's fix in place too, "Boston" on "under 500,000" is still Boston (Lincolnshire) (the in-cohort top holder, Boston MA, is as famous as the capital). Change the `startsA` assertion in `tests/namesakes.test.js` to expect the nudge.
