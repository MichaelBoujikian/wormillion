# West and Central Asia islands — 2026-09-19 (the West and Central Asia wave, probe 5)

Branch `expansion-2`. Probe `scripts/expansion/probes/wca-islands.json` (the
25 countries' island categories, 12 national lists; floor 1 km²,
`noFigureViews` 122, views floor 91); chunk files in `work/folded/wca/`
(`new-islands-wca5.txt`, `new-islands-ns-wca5.txt`). Bank 19,749 →

Ekinlik — were kept out of it; a human's call).

## Method

- Tree: 58 categories, 289 pages; 12 lists. **"List of islands of Turkey"
  (321 links) resolved to the article *Turkey*** — the country's whole
  navbox (Economy of Turkey, Turkish Airlines, LGBTQ rights in Turkey…)
  came in as candidates at up to 265,000 views; the description rule cut
  them all (the third generic-page list this wave). 404 read as islands
  (254 skipped); 33 present, 2 taken, 6 fuzzy, 223 missing.
- Sizes: Wikidata and the article agreed on 44, the article overrode 9
  (Abd al Kuri 133 vs Wikidata's 4,562 — the archipelago's figure; Samhah
  40 vs 2,431; Darsah 5.4 vs 1,094; Farasan Island 380 vs 686; İmralı 9.98
  vs 13.3), the article alone sized 10, Wikidata alone 23; the sister pass
  sized 9 (ruwiki's Uzunada 25.39, trwiki's Ekinlik 2.47; arwiki's Qit'at
  Jaradah "48 م²" is 48 *square metres* — cut; enwiki's own Tavşan Islet
  "0,025" km² was read as 25 — cut; the decimal-comma class again). 147
  have no figure anywhere; 17 at 122+ views went in at `size` 0 (Gemiler,
  Sedir, Kara Ada, Yassi Ada, Orak, Eşek, Sıçan, Bluewaters, Siniyah,
  Jubail, Arzanah, Umm al-Fayyarin, Abadan Island, Adsiz Ada, Adır,
  Korzhin…).
- Floor 1 km²: 48 under it; Suluada (0.1 km², 1,431 views) lifted back.
  Views floor 91: 5 rows under it (Göcek Island, Nurana, Ukatny (Russia's),
  Buneh, Mardaliç).
