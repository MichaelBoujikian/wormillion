# United States × cities under 100,000 — coverage probe and fill (2026-09-16)

Last chunk of the US wave. The 2026-09-15 probe covered cities over 100,000
(`2026-09-15-us-cities.md`); this one takes the floor to **50,000** (the
user's number for this wave). Sources: the two national lists plus the 50
state "List of municipalities in X" / "List of cities in X" pages and the
census-designated-place lists that exist (Alaska, Maryland, Virginia,
Hawaii…) — 75 list pages, **42,857 candidate titles**, 30,575 read as
cities/towns/villages/CDPs. Population from Wikidata P1082 (the census
figure; no article cross-check was needed for cities). Config
`probes/us-cities-50k.json`.

The probe died once: the raw Wikidata answers for 30,000 places grew the
cache to 536 MB and `JSON.stringify` refused. `probe.mjs` no longer caches
those raw (the per-QID memo keeps the figure), and it resumed from the memo.
