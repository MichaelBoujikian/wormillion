# Canada rivers — 2026-09-18 (the Mexico and Canada wave, probe 2)

Branch `expansion-2`. Probe `scripts/expansion/probes/ca-rivers.json`; chunk
files in `work/folded/mx-ca/` (`new-rivers-ca.txt`, `new-rivers-ns-ca.txt`,
`qualify-ns-ca.txt`). Bank 15,520 → **15,945** (+360 rivers, +65 namesakes);
16 incumbents qualified. River cohort 3,995 → 4,420; jackpot share 1.85% →
1.67% (the line stays at 28.5 views).

## Method

- Tree: `Category:Rivers of Canada by province or territory` down through
  Ontario's census divisions and Quebec's regions (92 categories, 2,797
  pages); 15 lists (Canada, longest, each province — Quebec's alone has
  1,250 links). 3,851 candidates → 2,849 read as rivers.
- Floor 80 km (the US floor; same scale), `famousViews` 1000 (lifted Don
  River (Ontario), 38 km, 1,400 views). Wikidata sized 1,659; the enwiki
  article pass added 322 and overrode 103 (the frwiki-import decimal shift:
  Amyot 11.7 vs 117, Chimenticook 32.6 vs 326, Roaring 15 vs 167…).
  `--sister=fr` was tried for the 722 still unsized: only 36 have a frwiki
  article, 6 gave a length (Wascana Creek 250, Welland 135) — Canada's
  unsized rivers are enwiki-only creeks, unlike Mexico's.
- **Decision on the spot:** `noFigureViews: 122` (the islands' precedent
  for unsized rows, not Mexico's 30): 36 unsized rivers with 122+ views
  came in at `size` 0 (Speed, Ganaraska, Holland, Eramosa, Conestogo,
  Blaeberry, Black Creek (Toronto), Twelve Mile Creek, Duffins Creek,
  Johnston Creek, Fitzsimmons Creek, Whitemud Creek, Divide Creek, Yaak…).
- Chunk `--min-views=30 --allow-no-figure`, theme `North America`; 99 rows
  under 30 views left out; then `--taken-only` for the 84 name-taken rows
  (67 over 30 views).

## Left out, and why

- Concept / navbox rows: River, Waterfall, Stream, Tributary, Drainage
  system, Main stem, Executive Council of Alberta, Toronto waterway
  system, Laurentian River System, Malaspina Strait, Mill Creek Ravine.
- Settlements standing in for their creek: Furry Creek, Soda Creek,
  Saunders Creek (Alberta), Oyster River (British Columbia); Grey River,
  Newfoundland and Labrador (a local service district — `--check` caught
  it after the fold; dropped; Grey (New Zealand) keeps the qualifier it
  gained, the sole holder).
- Same name, same province, no clean qualifier: Rat River (Burntwood
  tributary) beside Rat (Red River tributary), both Manitoba; Salmon River
  (Fraser tributary) beside Salmon (British Columbia) (Shuswap's, more
  viewed).
- 99 sized rivers under 30 views/mo; 17 taken rows under 30 views
  (Sturgeon (Manitoba), Root, Broad, Fox (Cochrane District), Smith,
  Swift, Blue, Cottonwood, Boyer, Sand, Otter, Seal, Poplar (Fawn), three
  Beavers, Clyde (Alberta)); 680 unsized under 122 views; 1,458 below 80 km.

## For a human

- Raven River (Alberta): article 450 km vs Wikidata 95 — the article won by
  rule; 95 looks right for a Red Deer tributary. Same class: Little Red
  Deer 315 vs 165, Stuart 187 vs 110, Brightwater Creek 270 km.
- Wascana Creek 250 km and Welland 135 km are frwiki figures.
- Pembina's incumbent went to Manitoba (the title's first-named), Poplar's
  to Montana, Churchill River's to Saskatchewan — the two-country rivers
  follow the "state most of it runs through" rule as best I could tell.
- Fox / Vermilion / Sturgeon / Black River took "(Ontario)" from their
  district descriptions; a second Ontario namesake later means a district
  qualifier for both (qualify.mjs renames).
- `--check` refused ten rivers for no short description (Big Salmon,
  Sikanni Chief, Bay du Nord, Klappan, Nation, Ospika, Rivière aux Mélèzes,
  Ross, Sustut, Terra Nova) and two namesakes (Salmon (Vancouver Island),
  Rancheria (Yukon)); all verified by hand.
