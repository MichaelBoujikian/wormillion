# West and Central Asia lakes — 2026-09-19 (the West and Central Asia wave, probe 3)

Branch `expansion-2`. Probe `scripts/expansion/probes/wca-lakes.json` (the
25 countries' lake and reservoir categories, 25 national lists; floor
25 km², `famousViews` 1000); chunk file `work/folded/wca/new-lakes-wca3.txt`.
Bank 19,642 → **19,693** (+51 lakes; no namesakes). Lakes have no regional
theme; 11 salt lakes went into `saltwater`.

## Method

- Tree: 30 roots (lakes + reservoirs) and their subcategories; 25 lists.
  **Fourteen of the national lists resolved to the same 362-link worldwide
  page** (Iran's, Saudi Arabia's, Iraq's, Syria's, Lebanon's, Jordan's,
  Yemen's, Oman's, the UAE's, Qatar's, Bahrain's, Kuwait's, Kyrgyzstan's,
  Cyprus's — countries without a list of their own redirect to "List of
  lakes by country" or the like), so Suriname's Brokopondo, Chile's
  O'Higgins/San Martín, Benin's Ahémé, Madagascar's Ihotry, Cameroon's
  Lagdo, South Sudan's Lake No, El Salvador's Cerrón Grande, Guatemala's
  Güija, Panama's Bayano, Antarctica's Don Juan Pond and Whillans, India's
  Chandra Taal / Tsomgo / Shanti Sagara, the Great Lakes and the Laccadive
  Sea came in as candidates — all cut by description (the same lesson as
  the rivers' Bahrain list; the ones over the floor are gaps for their own
  waves: O'Higgins/San Martín 1,013 km² at 1,065 views, Brokopondo 1,560,
  Lagdo 586, Bayano 353, Cochrane/Pueyrredón 325).
- 696 read as lakes (1,542 skipped); 188 present, 1 taken, 4 fuzzy, 151
  missing after the article pass. Sizes: the first article pass under
  `--min-views` read nothing unsized (the probe fetches no views for rows
  without a figure when the config has no `noFigureViews`), so the pass
  was rerun over **all 419** unsized-or-in-scope rows: the article alone
  sized **232**, agreed with Wikidata on 48, overrode 3 (Kushmurun 415 vs
  210, Mehman 12 vs 35); the sister pass
  (`--sister=ru,tr,fa,ar,he,ka,hy,az,kk,uz`) sized 22 more (ruwiki's
  Kapchagay 1,847, Tuzkan 705, Gavkhouni 476, Qadisiyah 500, Sardoba 60;
  kkwiki's Aqköl 62). 103 have no figure anywhere and stayed out (lakes
  are never unsized).
- Floor 25 km²: 249 under it; five lifted back by 1,000+ views — Uzungöl
  (0.13 km², 1,187 views) and Shagan (5.2 km², 1,126 — the "Atomic Lake"
  of the 1965 test) went in; Chandra Taal, Don Juan Pond and Tsomgo are
  foreign. Views floor 61: 81 sized rows under it left out (Kazakhstan's
  steppe lakes at 30 views: Karasor 155 km², Kushmurun 415, Shalkarteniz
  1,800, Siletiteniz 777, Sarykopa 184, Shalkar 206 — a Kazakhstan salt
  lake pass at a 30 floor would find twenty more).

## Left out, and why

- The 23 foreign rows above; the South Aral Sea (dried); Godzareh
  Depression (2,417 km² by Wikidata — Afghanistan's usually-dry salt
  flat, "Lake" only by Wikidata's description; a human may want it in
  `saltwater`); **Al-Asfar Lake** (arwiki's "250,000 م²" was read as
  250,000 km² — the field is square metres: 0.25 km², under the floor;
  the sister parser has no unit check — noted for `article-size.mjs`);
  **Lake Kuyucuk** (the article's "245" is hectares: 2.45 km²).
- Alakol (Astrakhan District) (26.3 km², 30 views): a loose-held namesake
  of Lake Alakol under the views floor; Mamay (20 views, fuzzy to Lake
  Mamry).
- 81 rows under 61 views; 103 unsized.

## For a human

- Barsakelmes Lake 450 km² is Wikidata's only figure (a salt lake in the
  Aral's former bed, in `saltwater`); Kapchagay 1,847 km² and Tuzkan 705
  are ruwiki's; Lake Hammar 600 is the article's against Wikidata's
  1,300 (the marsh lake varies by season). For the audit.
- A sister figure's unit: arwiki's Al-Asfar "250,000 م²" and the
  article's Kuyucuk "245" (ha) both read as km²; both were caught by eye
  (a 250,000 km² lake in Saudi Arabia). `article-size.mjs` could refuse a
  lake over 100,000 km² and flag "م²" / "ha" / "hectare" fields — not done
  this session.
- The national list of every small country without lakes redirects to a
  worldwide list — the second time this wave. The probe could print a
  warning when two lists resolve to the same page (not done).
