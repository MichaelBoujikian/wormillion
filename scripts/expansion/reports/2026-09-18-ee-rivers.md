## Method

- Tree: the 21 `Rivers of <country>` categories down through their
  subdivisions (216 categories, 3,673 pages; Romania's mass-created stubs are
  a third of it); the 21 national lists. 7,294 candidates → 3,658 read as
  rivers; 472 missing, 43 fuzzy, 16 taken.
- Floor 60 km (Europe's), `famousViews` 1000. Wikidata sized 1,636; the
  enwiki article pass sized **1,515 more** and overrode 43 (the frwiki /
  rowiki decimal-shift class: Hoczewka 27.8 vs 278, Rudawa 35.4 vs 354, Skrapež
  47.7 vs 477); `--sister=ro,pl,uk,cs,bg,sr,hr,el,lt,et,lv,hu,sk,sl,ru` (new:
  several sister wikis in turn, each with its own infobox template and field
  name) sized 35 of the 239 still unsized, nearly all under the floor. 204
  rivers have no figure anywhere and stayed out (the Europe rule; no
  `noFigureViews`).
- Chunk `--min-views=30`, theme `Europe`; 125 sized rivers under 30 views
  left out; three rows whose fresh 60-day median came in under 30 (the
  probe's window had them at 30) dropped after the fold.
