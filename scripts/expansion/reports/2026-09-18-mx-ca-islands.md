## Method

- Tree: `Islands of Mexico` (by state, the lake / Pacific / atoll / artificial
  subcategories), `Islands of Canada by province or territory` down through
  BC's Gulf Islands / Haida Gwaii / Discovery Islands and Nunavut's regions,
  the Arctic Archipelago, the Labrador Sea and the North Atlantic islands of
  Canada (96 categories, 1,379 pages); 9 lists. 2,270 candidates → 1,385
  read as islands.
- Floor 1 km², `famousViews` 1000 (lifted Hans Island, Beechey, Granville
  (dropped), Spirit Island, Flowerpot…), `noFigureViews` 122 (155 unsized
  kept). The article pass filled 46 and overrode 5 (Clarion Island 19.8 vs
  Wikidata's 1,613 — the reserve; San Pedro Mártir 2.72 vs 290). Views floor
  91 at the chunk.
- New engine word: **`isla` / `islas` are island words for the matcher**
  (Isla Ángel de la Guarda answers "Angel de la Guarda") and letters for the
  letter rules, like `rio` and `lago`.
