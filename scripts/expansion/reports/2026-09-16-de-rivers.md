# Germany, Austria, Switzerland, Liechtenstein, Luxembourg × rivers — coverage probe and fill (2026-09-16)

Second chunk of the Europe wave. Method: every article under `Category:Rivers
of Germany` (the flat 500+ page category plus `…by state`), `…of Austria` (+
`by state`), `…of Switzerland` (+ `by canton`), `…of Liechtenstein` and `…of
Luxembourg` (76 categories, 3,838 pages) plus the links of 21 list pages
(the national lists and the sixteen German state lists) — 4,550 candidate
titles, 3,345 resolved to river articles. **Floor 60 km** (between the UK's
50 and the US's 80; continental rivers are longer than Britain's and there
are far more of them), `famousViews` 1000. Wikidata's P2043 covered 3,023 of
the 3,345 (German rivers are well kept in Wikidata) and the article pass read
the 486 in-scope or unsized ones: 35 disagreements, all to the article —
the **frwiki/dewiki decimal shift** is real (Aist 138 → 13.7, Cassarate 183
→ 18.3, Moesa 472 → 47, Glogn 307 → 30.7, Veyron 185 → 18.5). Config:
`probes/de-rivers.json`.

  Munich's river-surfing wave) and **Breg** (46 km, 2,131 — the Danube's

  "Möhne" lands. **"Moehne" does not** — the ue/oe/ae transliteration is a
  matcher gap (Munich's alias is "Munchen", not "Muenchen"); noted below.
- Themes: all 125 in `Europe`. `gap-check` pins 18 headline names. Bank
  12,187; river cohort 3,374 (was 3,249); jackpot share 2.7%.

## Decisions taken on the spot

- **Floor 60 km.** At 80 the chunk would have been ~70 rows and the Emscher
  (83), Werre (72), Möhne (65), Schwentine (62) — rivers with their own
  fame — would have stayed traps; at 50 another ~400 sub-100-view streams
  would have come in. 60 for the rest of continental Europe and the Nordics.
- **Sections are not rivers.** "Middle Rhine" (1,065 views) is a famous
  article, but on a river round the answer is the Rhine.
- **Rhin stays out** even though it is a real river: the bare name is the
  Rhine's French name.

## What the probe found on the way

- Pass 1 died once with exit code 1 and no message during the views fetch
  (2,738 titles, throttled); the re-run resumed from the cache in a minute.
  The `famousViews` fetch over ~2,700 below-floor titles is the slow part
  (~1 h) — it found two rivers. Worth keeping (the two are the most viewed
  of the chunk), but the cost is known now.
- Every fold of this chunk was offline; the only network was
  `fetch-pageviews` for the 128 new/re-pointed rows.

## Judgment calls for a human

- **ue/oe/ae transliteration**: "Muenchen", "Moehne", "Wuemme", "Koeln" do
  not match — `normalize` strips diacritics but does not fold the German
  digraphs. A one-line rule in `matching.normalize` (ae/oe/ue → a/o/u only
  for entries whose original name carries an umlaut, or as an alias policy
  at fold time) would fix it; it has a side effect on names that genuinely
  contain "ue" (Duero, Guernsey → "Gernsey"?). Recommend: aliases at fold
  time for names with umlauts, not a normalize rule.
- The ten namesakes (Kinzig (Rhine) 93 km / 244 views vs the bank's Kinzig
  (Main) 86 km / 122; Thur was fixed; Ill (Vorarlberg) vs Ill (France)) —
  decision 5's pile grows.
