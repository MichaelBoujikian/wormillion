  subcategories (97 categories, 673 pages); the lists of islands of China,
  Japan (by area too), the Koreas, Taiwan and Hong Kong. 1,748 candidates
  → 626 read as islands; 115 present, 6 taken, 27 fuzzy, 230 missing.
- Sizes: Wikidata had an area for 245; the article pass sized 27 more and
