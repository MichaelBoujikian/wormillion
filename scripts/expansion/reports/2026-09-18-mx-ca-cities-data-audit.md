## Confirmed findings

### F1. St. Catharines (city-st-catharines, 8,645 views) — its commonest misspelling "St. Catherines" is refused with an island nudge
- What is wrong: "St. Catherines" / "St Catherines" / "Saint Catherines" (the spelling most people outside the Niagara region use; enwiki keeps the redirect "St. Catherines, Ontario") is corrected to St. Catharines by the matcher (one edit), but run.js ranks a loose name in another cohort above an in-cohort correction, and the island cohort's St. Catherines Island (Georgia) holds the loose form "st catherines" — so the city round answers "that's an island".
- Evidence: try.mjs city: "St. Catherines" → elsewhere:island:island-st-catherines-island; "St Catherines", "Saint Catherines" → the same; "St. Catharines" → accepted:city-st-catharines. matching.matchAnswer("St. Catherines", city lookup) → {status:"corrected", entryId:"city-st-catharines"} — the correction exists and is overruled by the nudge (run.js submit: "only an exact or loose name in another cohort may nudge" — the island's loose form is exactly that). Pre-wave the same typing gave the same nudge (the city was not in the bank), so nothing got worse, but the row is new and this is its main typed form.
- What the player sees: on "Name a city…", "St. Catherines" is refused with "St. Catherines Island is an island"; only the exact "St. Catharines" scores.
- Fix: alias `St. Catherines` on the row (scripts/data-cities.mjs:3995 `St. Catharines|Canada|136803|St. Catherines`) — an exact key beats the nudge; "Saint Catherines" then lands through the saint/st normalisation.
- Severity: wrong refusal of a new row's common spelling (alias).

### F2. Four municipality articles folded as cities, on the municipality's population, where the seat is a different or far smaller town
- What is wrong: the probe report says the 40 "X Municipality" articles and "five municipalities whose seats are the cities" were left out; these four are the same class under a plain title. Wikidata's P31 for each is Q1952852 "municipality of Mexico" (not Q20202352 "locality"), and the article's own lead says so.
  - **La Paz (State of Mexico)** (city-la-paz-state-of-mexico, 304,088, 122 views): "La Paz is a municipality in the State of Mexico, Mexico, with its municipal seat in the town of Los Reyes Acaquilpan" (the place everybody calls Los Reyes La Paz; "Los Reyes Acaquilpan" redirects to this article). 304,088 is the 2020 municipality (Wikidata @2020, no census qualifier); the infobox is the 2010 municipality (253,845) with the seat at 85,359. try.mjs city: "Los Reyes La Paz", "Los Reyes Acaquilpan", "Los Reyes" → unrecognized. The row is reachable only as "La Paz, State of Mexico" (the bare "La Paz" goes to Bolivia's, then Baja California Sur's), and at 122 views it is the rarest La Paz.
  - **Purísima del Rincón** (city-purisima-del-rincon, 68,795, 244 views): "Purísima del Rincón is a Mexican municipality … Its municipal seat is the town of Purísima de Bustos"; 68,795 is the 2010 municipality (Wikidata @2010; the article: "68,795 inhabitants according to the 2010 census"); no figure for the seat in the article. "Purisima de Bustos" typed → unrecognized.
  - **Yautepec** (city-yautepec, 105,780, 213 views): "Yautepec is a municipality … The municipal seat is the city of Yautepec de Zaragoza … In the 2020 census the municipality had a population of 105,780 … Yautepec de Zaragoza had 44,353 inhabitants in 2020." The city is under the wave's 50,000 floor by 5,600; the bank carries 2.4× its population.
  - **Ocosingo** (city-ocosingo, 234,661, 274 views): infobox `settlement_type = Municipality`, `population_total = 234661` (2020 census), `population_blank1_title = City`, `population_blank1 = 47688`. The town is under the floor; the bank carries 4.9× its population (the municipality is the size of a small country — it reaches the Guatemalan border).
- Evidence: mxca-city-wikitext.json leads and infoboxes as quoted; mxca-city-wikidata.out P31 = Q1952852 for all four; work/mx-ca-cities.json descriptions "Municipality in State of Mexico, Mexico" / "Mexican municipality located in the state of Guanajuato" / "Municipality in Morelos, Mexico" / "Municipality in Chiapas, Mexico".
- What the player sees: nothing crosses 500,000, so the size prompts are unaffected; the review shows Ocosingo as a 235k city and Yautepec as a 106k one; "Los Reyes La Paz" is unknown to the game while its municipality is in under a name nobody uses for the town.
- Fix: drop Yautepec and Ocosingo (their towns are under the 50,000 floor: `drop.mjs` city-yautepec, city-ocosingo; data-cities.mjs:4111, 4096), or re-size them to 44,353 / 47,688 with a WIKI_VERIFIED note if the lead prefers to keep the names. Purísima del Rincón: drop (2010 figure, seat under the floor by every estimate I know, not checkable offline), or keep with alias `Purisima de Bustos`. La Paz (State of Mexico): either drop the row (122 views, unreachable bare) or keep it as the municipality with aliases `Los Reyes La Paz,Los Reyes Acaquilpan,Los Reyes` (data-cities.mjs:4128) so the town's name lands. For the pipeline: a candidate whose Wikidata P31 is Q1952852 (municipality of Mexico) and not also Q20202352 / Q515 is the "X Municipality" class whatever its title.
- Severity: wrong subject / wrong figure (the class the report says it excluded); two rows below the floor.

### F3. Eight city rows carry a municipality figure (2010–2020) where the article gives the seat's own

  - **Cortazar** (city-cortazar) 88,397 = municipality 2010 (Wikidata @2010); infobox 2020 `population_total = 69371` (city), `Municipality = 97928`. → 69,371.
  - **Santa Cruz Xoxocotlán** (city-santa-cruz-xoxocotlan) 93,188 = municipality 2015; infobox 2020 municipality 100,402, `Seat = 81848`. → 81,848.

  - **Zumpango** (city-zumpango) 159,647 = municipality 2010 (Wikidata @2010); infobox 2020 municipality 280,455; the seat Zumpango de Ocampo has no figure in the article (2010: about 50,000). → the seat's 2020 figure from INEGI, or the row noted as a municipality.
  - **Tulancingo** (city-tulancingo) 161,069 = municipality 2015 (Wikidata @2015); infobox 2010 municipality 151,582; the seat (Tulancingo de Bravo) not given. → INEGI 2020 seat figure.
  - **San Pedro Cholula** (city-san-pedro-cholula) 129,032 = municipality 2015 (Wikidata @2015, preferred); infobox 2005: municipality 113,436, seat 82,964. → INEGI 2020 seat figure (see F4 for the alias).

  - (Tlalnepantla de Baz 700,734 is the 2015 municipality (Wikidata @2015) against 672,202 in 2020 — the city is coextensive and both sides are over 500,000; nothing to change.)

- What the player sees: review figures 1.3–1.7× the town's; no prompt outcome changes.
- Fix: the four numbers above (data-cities.mjs:4089, 4118, 4097, 4078), INEGI 2020 seat figures for Zumpango / Tulancingo / San Pedro Cholula / La Piedad (not in the articles; not checkable from Wikipedia alone), or a note that these rows are municipalities by design.
