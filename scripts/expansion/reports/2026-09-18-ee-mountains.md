(18 national lists + "List of highest points of European countries"; lists
only, no elevation floor, the 122-views floor); chunk files in

## Method

- 2,871 candidates → 705 read as mountains; 597 missing, 10 fuzzy, 5
  taken. Views for all 612 in scope; the article pass over the 169 at 122+
  views (eleven sister wikis for the 25 unsized: two figures). Chunk
  `--min-views=122 --allow-no-figure` (20 unsized at 0); 439 peaks under
  122 views left out.
- Themes: Ciomadul → `volcanoes` (Romania's only); the Julian, Kamnik–Savinja
  and Karawanks peaks → `the Alps` by `range-tag.mjs` (Krn, Škrlatica,
  Prisojnik, Razor, Skuta, Špik, Brana, Jalovec, Raduha, Mittagskogel,
  Petzen). **The range table's `\bAlps\b` had matched "Dinaric Alps"** and
  tagged 24 Dinaric peaks as the Alps; "Dinaric", "Albanian",
  "Transylvanian", "Apuseni", "Scandinavian", "Swabian" and "Franconian"
  Alps are excluded now and the 24 were stripped.
