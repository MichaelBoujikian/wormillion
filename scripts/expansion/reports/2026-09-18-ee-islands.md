## Method

- 87 categories, 746 pages (Greece's tree is most of it); 2,970 candidates
  → 727 read as islands; 264 missing. Floor 1 km², `famousViews` 1000 (Vido,
  Pontikonisi, Mamula, Imia, Margaret Island, Elafonisi…), `noFigureViews`
  122 (41 unsized at 0). Views floor 91.
- The sister pass (el, hr, et, uk, pl) exposed a bug: a Greek "0,357" was
  read as 357 km² (the sister rule turned only one- and two-digit decimals);
  a comma is the decimal there whatever follows it now, and the five rows it
  had inflated (Argyronisos, Agia Kyriaki, Kardiotissa, Psathoura, Afentis
  Christos) were hand-set. 33 ocean overrides: the Danube (Margaret, Csepel,
  Szentendre, Szigetköz, Žitný ostrov, Great War Island, Ada Ciganlija,
  Great Brăila, Belene, Šarengrad, Tătaru Mare), Vistula, Warta, Vltava,
  Dnieper and Daugava islands and the lake / lagoon islands in no ocean; six
  Mediterranean / Black Sea articles without coordinates placed by hand.
