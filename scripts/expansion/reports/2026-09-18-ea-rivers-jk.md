## Method

- Tree: the four `Rivers of <country>` categories and their prefecture /
  province subcategories (67 categories, 412 pages); the lists of rivers of
  Japan, Korea (the same page under three titles) and Taiwan (288 links,
  many of them the island's government and language pages). 674 candidates
  → 411 read as rivers; 42 present, 8 taken, 16 fuzzy, 111 missing.
- Sizes: Wikidata had a length for 111; the article pass sized 20 more and
  overrode 4; `--sister=ja,ko,zh` sized 12 (jawiki's 延長, kowiki's 길이).
  147 rivers under 60 km (Japan's are short) and 87 unsized under 122 views
  stayed out; 10 unsized at 122+ went in at `size` 0 (Isuzu, Pothong…).
- Themes by description: Japan's rivers → `Japan`, the Koreas' → `Korea`,
  Taiwan's → none (no Taiwan river theme; not `China`).
