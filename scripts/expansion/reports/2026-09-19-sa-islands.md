# South America islands — 2026-09-19 (the South America wave, probe 5)

Branch `expansion-2`. Probe `scripts/expansion/probes/sa-islands.json` (the
13 countries' island categories, 13 national lists; floor 1 km²,
`noFigureViews` 122, views floor 91); chunk files in `work/folded/sa/`
(`new-islands-sa5.txt`, `new-islands-ns-sa5.txt`, `qualify-ns-sa5.txt`). Bank
20,609 → **20,658** (+45 islands, +4 namesakes); 2 incumbents qualified, 2
re-pointed, 1 re-sized. Four Caribbean-coast islands into `the Caribbean`.

## Method

- Tree: 50 categories, 258 pages; 13 lists — seven of them (Colombia,
  Venezuela, Ecuador, Suriname, Guyana, Bolivia, Paraguay) resolve to one
  268-link worldwide page, and French Guiana's to the 516-link "Islands of
  France" (French Polynesia's atolls, the Kerguelen, New Caledonia — 83
  foreign rows cut by description). 521 read as islands; 120 present, 13
  taken, 12 fuzzy, 236 missing. Sizes: the article pass read 184 (agree
  78, override 20, article alone 10, Wikidata alone 34; `--sister=es,pt`
  6); 27 at 122+ views went in at `size` 0 (Águila Islet, Isla Bermejo,
  Elizabeth Island, Fuerte, Tintipán…).
- Floor 1 km²: 55 under it; five lifted back by fame — Ilha da Queimada
  Grande (Snake Island, 0.43 km², 19,664 views), El Ojo (the floating
  island, 0.01, 6,423), Motu Nui (0.04, 2,983), El Frontón (1). Views floor
  91: 83 rows under it (Chile's Patagonian channels' islands, the atolls).
- **Three incumbents were wrong**: the bank's *Tierra del Fuego*
  (47,992 km² — the main island's area) and *Chiloe* (8,394 — the island's)
  pointed at the archipelago articles and are re-pointed to "Isla Grande
  de Tierra del Fuego" and "Chiloé Island" (the names stay — players type
  them); *Santa Cruz Island* carried the Galápagos island's 986 km² on
  California's article — now Santa Cruz Island (California) at 250 km²,
  with **Santa Cruz Island (Galápagos)** (986) a namesake. Isla Grande de
  Tierra del Fuego and Chiloé Island therefore did not fold as new rows.
