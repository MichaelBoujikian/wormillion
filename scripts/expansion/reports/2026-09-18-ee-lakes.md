## Method

- 86 categories, 938 pages; 3,714 candidates → 1,063 read as lakes; 183
  present already (the Europe wave and the v1.1 fill had the famous ones),
  99 missing. Floor 25 km², `famousViews` 1000 (lifted eleven: Morskie Oko
  0.35 km² at 3,501 views, Bâlea Lake, Lake Kournas, Prokoško Lake…). The
  article pass sized 266 the Wikidata pass had nothing for; the fifteen
  sister wikis 45 more. Views floor 61.
- "List of lakes by country" dragged in 30 rows outside the wave (the Aral
  Seas, Sun Moon Lake, O'Higgins/San Martín, Lake No…) — left for their
  waves; the Great Lakes, the Adriatic Sea, Andros and a New Brunswick
  parish were navbox noise.
