## Method

- Tree: 77 categories, 725 pages; 16 lists — six of them (Colombia,
  Venezuela, Ecuador, Paraguay, Guyana, Suriname) resolve to the same
  362-link worldwide page (the West and Central Asia lesson), French
  Guiana's does not exist. 830 read as lakes; **200 present** (Titicaca,
  Poopó, Maracaibo, the Patagonian lakes, the Chilean lake district are
  the bank's), 2 taken (foreign), 4 fuzzy, 115 missing; 432 with no
  figure.
- With `noFigureViews` 61 in the config the unsized rows got views, so the
  article pass read the 125 in-scope-or-61-plus rows (agree 27, override 8,
  article alone 38; `--sister=es,pt` 4) — the West and Central Asia lakes
  lesson applied.
- Floor 25 km²: 77 under it; Lake Guatavita (0.2 km², 3,044 views — El
  Dorado's lake) and Laguna del Carbón (9 km², 1,309 — the lowest point of
  the Americas, a salt lake) lifted back by fame. Views floor 61: 46 rows
  under it.
