## Method

- Tree: 27 categories, 250 pages; six lists (Uruguay's 152 links,
  Ecuador's 99). 254 read as rivers; 58 present, 5 taken, 4 fuzzy, 39
  missing; 140 with no figure. The article pass read 78 (agree 9, override
  2, article only 2); `--sister=es,pt,fr,nl` sized 4 (the French Guiana
  rows from frwiki); 57 have no figure anywhere; 5 at 122+ views went in at
  `size` 0 (Pomeroon, Canje). Views floor 30: 18 sized rows under it
  (Uruguay's Caraguatá, San Salvador, Porongos, Rosario; Suriname's
  Oelemari, Koursibo).
- The hand-clean's CONCEPT pattern caught "River of Ecuador" (four real
  rivers: Esmeraldas, Guayllabamba, Chone, Zamora — kept by hand, and the
  pattern is now a known false positive for the Spanish-style
  description); the Viceroyalty of the Río de la Plata, the British
  invasions of the River Plate and the Uruguayan Air Force were cut.
