### C1. "Etna" / "Aso" on a non-river, non-mountain round now nudge to the 30-view river, not the volcano (check 4a/4b; `src/js/run.js` `elsewhere`, low)

`elsewhere(rawInput, category, exactOnly)` walks `bank.cohorts` in CATEGORIES order (country > capital > city > lake > **river** > mountain > desert > island > sea_ocean) and returns the first cohort with an exact hit. Mount Etna carries the alias `Etna` and Mount Aso the alias `Aso`; the wave adds rivers *named* Etna (Norway, 106 km, 30 views) and Aso (Italy, 30 views), so on every round that is neither a river nor a mountain round the river cohort is reached first:

| typed | round | old bank | new bank |
|---|---|---|---|
| Etna | lake / country / city / capital / desert / island / sea | "Mount Etna is a mountain — this round wants a lake" | **"Etna is a river — this round wants a lake"** |
| Aso | same seven rounds | "Mount Aso is a mountain — …" | **"Aso is a river — …"** |
| Etna / Aso | mountain round | accepted: Mount Etna / Mount Aso | unchanged |
| Etna / Aso | river round | the mountain nudge | accepted on the river (see J2) |
| Mount Etna, Mt Etna | any round | the mountain nudge / accepted | unchanged |

What the player sees: a free-retry hint that names a river nobody has heard of (42,433 views vs 30) instead of the volcano they typed. The other five twins (Fier, Bar, Ronne, Bienne — cities, 1,826–5,692 views; Glan — a 30-view lake) are unaffected because city and lake precede river in the order. Fix (rule, `run.js` `elsewhere`): on the exact pass collect every cohort's hit and return the entry with the largest `magnitude` rather than the first cohort's — Mount Etna 42,433 beats Etna 30, Mount Aso 3,348 beats Aso 30, and the city twins keep winning (Bar 5,692 vs 30, Biel 4,049 vs 30, Ronne 2,070 vs 30, Fier 1,826 vs 91); Glan flips to the 61-view river over the 30-view lake, which nobody will notice. Fix (data, narrower): drop the Norwegian Etna (`river-etna`, 30 views; the nordic report already lists it as a judgment call) — that leaves Aso.

### C2. Seven one-edit typos of famous rivers now score a 30-view new row (check 5a; `src/js/matching.js` `nearest`, medium)

`nearest` scores a candidate hit on the **full** key as `distance × 2` and a hit on the **stripped** (filler-removed) form as `distance × 2 + 1`, so "a hit on the full string beats a hit on the stripped form at the same distance" ("Lotse" is Lhotse, not a tie with Lose Hill). A famous river whose entry name carries a generic word — Negro River, Rio Grande, Saigon River, Tijuana River, Lhasa River — can only ever be reached through its stripped form, while a bare-named new row is reached through its full key, so at the same edit distance the obscure bare name wins outright instead of tying. ## Judgment calls

### J1. The 30-views floor is the right cut for the jackpot share; 61 buys nothing there (check 3)

The river cohort's rarity runs from 7 views (Mecaya, 100%) to 81,123 (0%), and rarity 0.85 falls at **28.5 views**: a row scores a jackpot only under 29 views. The 91 rows dropped in 19a5183 sat at 8–29 views (90 jackpots; Alagnon at 29 was 84.8%) and every one of the 459 survivors is at 30 or more, so the wave now contributes **zero** jackpots: 82 -> 81 (Eder (Eggel), 18 views, re-pointed to Eder (Fulda), 335) over a cohort of 3,249 -> 3,708, share 2.52% -> 2.18% (pre-drop: 171 / 3,799 = 4.50%; the lead's three numbers reproduce). A wave floor of 61 would drop 269 more rows — 59% of the wave, every one at exactly 30 views — and leave the jackpot count at 81; the share would read 2.36% purely because the denominator shrank (91: 2.42%, 122: 2.45%). Applying any floor to the *whole* cohort instead would be the mountains trap (a cohort minimum of 30 puts 33.8% of the cohort at jackpot).

What 61 *would* change is the pile at exactly 30 views/mo: rarity 84.45%, 800 points, dig 59.1 — 0.55 points and one view/day under the jackpot cliff (28 views = 85.2% = 950 points, dig 75). The old bank had 292 rivers there (9.0% of the cohort); the new one has 559 (15.1%), and 325 of the 1,466 rivers "Name a river in Europe" accepts sit on it (its ★ is still Crna, 12 views). That cliff is pre-existing (the previous auditors called it J2), the wave nearly doubles its width, and it is the same 800-point outcome for the player either way. Not a problem to fix in the data; if the pile ever matters it is a scoring-curve question (`rarity.js` JACKPOT_RARITY / the quantised medians), not a floor question.
