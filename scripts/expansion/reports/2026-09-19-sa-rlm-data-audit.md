### F1. Manso (Mato Grosso) (river-manso-mato-grosso / "Manso River (Mato Grosso)", 850 km, 30 views) carries the whole Cuiabá system's length on a headstream — over the 500 km threshold for a reach of ~300 km
- What is wrong: the Manso is the upper course / main headstream of the Cuiabá (its infobox: `river_system = [[Cuiabá River]]`, `mouth_coordinates` -14.697, -56.241 — the confluence above Rosário Oeste where the Cuiabá proper begins; `length = {{convert|850|km}}` cited to the FAO's "Inland waters of Latin America" (Ziesler & Ardizzone 1979), the same table that gives the Cuiabá 828 km on Wikidata). The wave's own Cuiabá row (river-cuiaba) is 480 km by its article (the Cuiabá below the confluence; Wikidata 828 for the whole river from the Manso's source). 850 (Manso) + 480 (Cuiabá below the confluence) = 1,330 km, but the whole Cuiabá is 828 km by the same source family — so the Manso reach on its own is at most ~350 km (828 − 480), and the 850 is the whole-river figure repeated on the reach's article (the West and Central Asia audit's Qarah Aghaj / Mond class). The Manso's own source-to-confluence distance is ~130 km in a straight line.
- Evidence: work/wikitext-cache.json "Manso River (Mato Grosso)" and "Cuiabá River" (blocks above); work/sa-rivers-big-sizes.json: Manso "article only (length: {{convert|850|km}}<ref name=fao>...)", Cuiabá "article 480 vs wikidata 828"; work/sa-rivers-big.json: Manso description "River in Brazil", qid Q16917848, Cuiabá qid Q998087; src/data/rivers.json river-manso-mato-grosso {size 850, lat -14.697, lon -56.241}, river-cuiaba {size 480, lat -17.159, lon -56.644}. The Manso reach's own length is not checkable offline (ptwiki is not cached).
- What the player sees: "Manso" on "Name a river longer than 500 km" is accepted (the qualifier picks the Mato Grosso one because the Patagonian Manso is 110 km) for a river whose own reach is ~300 km; "Cuiabá" is refused on the same prompt for the river whose whole length (828 km) the Manso figure describes.
- Fix: re-size river-manso-mato-grosso — `Manso (Mato Grosso)|0` (answers no threshold prompt) or a verified reach figure with a WIKI_VERIFIED note (scripts/data-physical.mjs row `Manso (Mato Grosso)|850`, the chunk line); and consider authoring the Cuiabá as the range `480-828` so the whole-river figure answers "longer than 500 km" (the Amur precedent in satisfiesSize).
- Severity: wrong figure (a whole-river figure on a headstream) on the wrong side of the 500 km threshold.

### F2. Gravataí (river-gravatai / "Gravataí River", 640 km, 30 views) — Wikidata's 640 km is impossible for a river that runs entirely inside the Porto Alegre metropolitan area; over the 500 km threshold
- What is wrong: the only figure is Wikidata's 640 (`"wikidata only (no infobox figure)"`; the enwiki infobox has no length at all). The Gravataí rises in the Banhado Grande wetland (Santo Antônio da Patrulha / Glorinha, ~29.85 S 50.65 W) and reaches the Guaíba at Porto Alegre (the infobox mouth 29°58'S 51°12'W — the bank's coordinates) about 55 km away in a straight line; its whole basin is ~2,000 km² (it drains Gravataí, Cachoeirinha, Canoas, Alvorada). The river is a few tens of kilometres long (the Portuguese sources give ~40 km, ~100 km counting every meander — from memory, not checkable offline). A 640 km river cannot fit — the figure is either a decimal shift (64 km) or another river's. 640 would make the Gravataí longer than the bank's Jacuí-basin rivers Caí (285) and Taquari (Rio Grande do Sul) (520).
- Evidence: work/wikitext-cache.json "Gravataí River" (source1_location "Rio Grande do Sul state", mouth Guaíba River, no length); work/sa-rivers-big-sizes.json {wikidata 640, article null}; scripts/.cache/titles.json lat -29.9667 lon -51.2; src/data/rivers.json river-gravatai size 640; scripts/data-physical.mjs `Gravataí|640`. The exact length is not checkable offline.
- What the player sees: "Gravataí" accepted on "Name a river longer than 500 km" and refused on "shorter than 500 km".
- Fix: re-size river-gravatai to a verified figure with a WIKI_VERIFIED note, or `Gravataí|0` (it stays above the 80 km floor only on the famous exemption, so at ~40 km the honest fix is to drop the row: scripts/data-physical.mjs, the chunk line, the `Gravataí` entry in `South America` in src/data/themes.js). A parser note for article-size.mjs / the probe: a Wikidata-only length whose mouth and source coordinates are under 100 km apart and over 500 km long needs a sanity check (the Mehran / Belica class).
- Severity: wrong figure on the wrong side of the 500 km threshold (both directions).

### F3. Aquidauana (river-aquidauana / "Aquidauana River", 1,200 km, 30 views) — the enwiki infobox and Wikidata both say 1,200 km for a ~600 km tributary of a 490 km river; over the 1,000 km and 500 km thresholds

The probe report's own flag ("the river is ~600 km — the figure may be the basin's or the Miranda's"); the wave's usual second opinion (ptwiki) is not cached offline. 1,200 also puts the row over 1,000 km, the class the Mehran finding (WCA F3) covered.
