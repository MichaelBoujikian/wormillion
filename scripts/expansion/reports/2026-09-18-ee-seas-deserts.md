Branch `expansion-2`. Probes `scripts/expansion/probes/ee-seas.json`,
`ee-seas-2.json` (a second pass over the Black Sea basin categories) and
`ee-deserts.json`; chunk files in `work/folded/ee/` (`new-seas-ee.txt`).

## Seas — method

- Tree: the bays / gulfs / lagoons / straits categories of the 21 countries
  that have them, the Baltic / Black / Adriatic / Aegean / Ionian Sea
  categories and the seas of the Atlantic (47 categories, 445 pages); the
  worldwide lists of straits, gulfs and seas (the bays list and two Baltic
  lists do not exist). 1,655 candidates → 877 read as seas; 352 missing.
  Almost none has an area: `--allow-no-figure` at the standing 213-views
  floor; the article pass (`--sister=el,hr,pl,uk,bg,ro,et,lv,lt,sl,ru,sr`)
  sized 39 (Tallinn Bay 300 km² from lvwiki; the Gulf of Patras, the Bay of
  Puck, the Bay of Vlorë from enwiki).
- **The worldwide lists dragged in 227 rows outside the wave** (Ha Long Bay,
  Walvis Bay, the Tsushima Strait, Hauraki Gulf, Neva Bay…): cut to the rows
  whose description, title or source category names one of the 21 countries
  or their seas — the Mexico/Canada rule.
- The first probe's category roots were the national ones; the Black Sea's
  estuaries (limans) live under `Estuaries of Ukraine` / `Bays of the Black
  Sea`, which no root reached. A second probe (`ee-seas-2.json`, 34
  categories) found the Dnieper–Bug and Dniester Estuaries and 16 more, most
  under the floor.
