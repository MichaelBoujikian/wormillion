# Mexico rivers — 2026-09-18 (the Mexico and Canada wave, probe 1)

Branch `expansion-2`. Probe `scripts/expansion/probes/mx-rivers.json`; chunk
files in `work/folded/mx-ca/` (`new-rivers-mx.txt`, `new-rivers-ns-mx.txt`,
`qualify-ns-mx.txt`). Bank 15,429 → **15,520** (+73 rivers, +18 namesakes);
8 incumbents qualified. River cohort 3,904 → 3,995; jackpot share 1.90% →
1.85% (the line stays at 28.5 views).

## Method

- Tree: `Category:Rivers of Mexico` + `…by state` (30 categories, 236
  pages); lists "List of rivers of Mexico" (356 links — the navbox drags in
  195 Mexico-topic articles, all skipped by `kind`) and "List of longest
  rivers of Mexico". 444 candidates → 236 read as rivers.
- Floor 60 km (Europe's; Mexico's tree is small), `famousViews` 1000.
  **Wikidata has a length for 12 of 236; the enwiki infobox for 24** — the
  mass-created Mexican river stubs carry an empty `length` field. New in
  this wave: `article-size.mjs --sister=es` follows the item's Wikidata
  sitelink to eswiki and reads *its* infobox (`Ficha de río`, `longitud`;
  "1.081" is a thousand there, "126,6" a decimal). 48 of the 181 unsized
  had an eswiki article; 21 gave a length (Tamesí 300, Tonalá 300, Bavispe
  371, Huaynamota 280, Cazones 145, Tuxpan 150…). One was nonsense (Actopan
  "2,001 km²" — a basin area in the length field) and was dropped by hand.
- **Decision on the spot:** 160 rivers still had no figure anywhere, so
  `noFigureViews: 30` (the river cohort's standing views floor) admits the
  unsized ones with 30+ views/mo at `size` 0. 60 qualified; 48 of them went
  in (the rest were taken / fuzzy / concept rows). They answer no size
  prompt. The alternative was leaving Mexico's rivers at 29 present + 25.
- Chunk `--min-views=30 --allow-no-figure`, theme `North America`; then
  `--taken-only` for the 20 name-taken rows.
