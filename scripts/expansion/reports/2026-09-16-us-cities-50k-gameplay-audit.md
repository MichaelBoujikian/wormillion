### C2. "Name a non-capital city in a country whose flag has both blue and white in it." is no longer drawable (check 2)

`drawFlagRule` refuses a rule that more than `MAX_ELIGIBLE_SHARE` (0.6) of the cohort satisfies. Every US city carries red, white and blue; the 408 new rows took "both blue and white" from 1,509 / 2,781 = 54.3% to 1,917 / 3,189 = **60.1%**. Through `promptFor` + the harness's drawability test (`n >= MIN_ELIGIBLE && n <= total * 0.6`): drawable on `2a2fef6`, not on the working tree. "Both blue and red" is at 54.6% (1,741) and will cross with the next ~300 US or French/British cities; "red and white" (71.9%), "blue" (66.0%), "red" (84.8%), "white" (84.1%) were already over the gate before the wave. This is HANDOFF decision 2's class; the consequence for the dailies is in check 6 (one day's draw changes). Fix options: raise `MAX_ELIGIBLE_SHARE` to 0.7 (the HANDOFF's recommendation), or accept that the two-colour flag prompt on cities is gone for good — every future US wave pushes it further.

### C4. "Bayou Teche doesn't start with B (Bayou doesn't count)": the letter-only rule swallows the leading word of ten rivers whose name it is (check 2r-B)

`LETTER_ONLY_FILLER.river` strips `bayou` and `arroyo` wherever they sit, but eight bayous and two arroyos in the bank carry the word first, the way Rio Grande carries "Rio" — and the commit kept `rio` counting for precisely that reason. Through `submit` on "Name a river that starts with B." / "…with A.", old → new: **Bayou Teche** (1,278 views) accepted → wrong-scope, feedback *Bayou Teche doesn't start with B (Bayou doesn't count) — try another*; **Bayou Lafourche** (1,157v), Bayou Macon, Bayou Pierre, Bayou Meto, Bayou DeView, Bayou des Arc likewise; **Arroyo Seco** (1,461v, now "starts with S") and Arroyo Colorado (now "starts with C") the same. Bayou Bartholomew survives on "B" by luck (Bartholomew). Nobody in Louisiana says Teche starts with T. The note is accurate about what the rule did, which is what makes it read wrongly. Fix: mirror the `rio` rule for a *leading* letter-only word — in `variantsOf` / `uncountedWords`, keep the first word when it is in `LETTER_ONLY_FILLER` and the name has more words (so "Bayou Teche" → `bayou teche`, "Sugar Creek" → `sugar`, "Buffalo Bayou" → `buffalo`); or list `bayou` and `arroyo` beside `rio` as name-words. Ten rows, two of them over 1,000 views.
