## Method

- 32 list titles tried, 23 exist (every country has at least one: the
  "List of cities and towns in X" pages; the "by population" variants
  mostly do not); `listCountry` maps each to its `data-countries.mjs` name
  (Czechia). 6,799 candidates → 3,699 read as cities; 3,043 under 50,000
  (the Polish, Czech and Romanian town lists are long), 190 with no figure
  (Serbian "town and municipality" articles, Sarajevo's municipalities,
  concept rows — none a city over 50,000), 171 missing.
- The 20 national capitals (Prague… Podgorica, Minsk) were cut before the
  fold (it refuses them anyway). Views floor 91: nothing was near it (the
  lowest is Petroupoli at 244).
- A city listed in two countries' lists takes the first list's country
  (Zagreb came out "Hungary" — a capital, cut); every folded row's country
  was checked against its description.
